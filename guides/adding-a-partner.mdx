---
title: Adding a Partner
description: Create a partner record, attach their sources, set which leads they can take, and invite their team to the partner portal.
---

Partners are the outside businesses you trade leads with, like affiliates and vendors who *send* you leads through their sources, and buyers you *sell* to by ping/post. This guide walks through setting one up from scratch. For everything the partner record can do, see [Partners](/features/partners).

## Prerequisites

- The **Admin** [role](/features/roles). Other roles don't see **Partners** in the sidebar.
- For a supplier, at least one [source](/features/sources) to attach.
- For a buyer, their ping and post specs (URLs, authentication, payload shape, and what their responses look like).
- The email addresses of the partner's people who'll sign in.

## 1. Create the partner

Click **Partners** in the sidebar, then **New partner**.

  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-create-light.webp" alt="New partner form with Basic Info, Duplicate checks, Lead Quotas, and Toggles sections" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-create-dark.webp" alt="New partner form with Basic Info, Duplicate checks, Lead Quotas, and Toggles sections" />
</Frame>

1. Enter the partner's **Name**, as it should appear in your partner list and their portal.
2. Leave **Offer Penalty** empty unless you want to nudge this partner down when they compete for a lead. It lowers their offer score by that percentage without changing what they're charged.
3. Under **Duplicate checks**, keep **Use tenant default** unless this partner needs different rules.
4. Add **Lead Quotas** if your agreement limits how many leads, or how much revenue, you send them per day, week, or month. Leave the fields blank for no limit.
5. Under **Toggles**, decide whether the partner can see returned statuses (**Show Returned Status in Portal**, on by default) and whether returned leads are left out of their payout total (**Exclude Returned Leads from Payout**, off by default). Match these to your contract.
6. Click **Create partner**.

You land on the partner's record, with tabs for **Overview**, **Payouts**, **Lead Eligibility**, **Sources**, and **Users**.

## 2. Assign their sources

If the partner sends you leads, attach the sources they post through so their portal shows those leads.

1. Open the **Sources** tab and click **Assign source**.
2. Pick a source. Only sources that aren't assigned to another partner are listed; a source belongs to one partner at a time.
3. Click **Assign source**. Repeat for each source.

<Warning>
Assignment is retroactive. If the source already has leads, Juiced warns you that the partner will see all of them, including leads created before you attached it. To keep history private, create a fresh source for the partner and assign that instead.
</Warning>

To move a source to a different partner, use **Unassign** on its row first.

## 3. Set lead eligibility (optional)

If you'll sell leads to this partner, decide which ones they can receive. Open the **Lead Eligibility** tab, build condition groups from lead fields, and click **Save conditions**.

With no conditions, the partner is eligible for every lead. Eligibility applies wherever the partner can compete, so use it for standing rules like "only auto insurance leads". Routing order belongs in your [distribution stages](/features/distribution).

## 4. Connect their ping and post (buyers only)

For a partner who buys leads, stay on the **Overview** tab, open **Ping/Post**, and click **Configure Ping** and **Configure Post**. Map the payload, tell Juiced how to read the response, and run a test before going live. [Setting up partner ping/post](/guides/setting-up-partner-ping-post) walks through it step by step.

## 5. Invite the partner's team

1. Open the **Users** tab and click **Invite user**.
2. Enter their **Email** and send it.

The invitee gets an email to accept and create their account, and joins as a partner admin with full access to the partner account. Invites that haven't been accepted yet show as **Pending**, with **Resend invite** (for when it landed in spam) and **Revoke invite** (the link stops working immediately).

Once they accept, they appear in the same list. **Remove** takes their access away right away, and **Reset 2FA and passkeys** helps someone who's lost their authenticator app or passkey.

## 6. Check the partner portal

Partner users sign in at `/partners/login` on your Juiced subdomain or [custom domain](/guides/adding-a-custom-domain). Their portal covers their dashboard, sources (with integration details for posting leads), pings, posts, leads, and logs, plus settings for their profile and password.

If a partner can't see leads they expect, check that their sources are assigned (Step 2).

## 7. Send them leads (optional)

To route leads to a buyer partner, turn on partners in a [distribution stage](/features/distribution). Switch on **Enable Partners** in the stage's partner settings, then include all partners or just the ones you choose. Lead quotas, eligibility, and duplicate checks from the earlier steps all apply on top.

<Tip>
  Looking for partners to buy or supply leads? The [Hybrid Agency Mastermind](https://www.skool.com/hybrid-agency-mastermind) includes a private buyer and seller network.
</Tip>

## Later changes

- **Edit** in the partner's header changes the name, penalty, duplicate checks, quotas, and toggles.
- The **Payouts** tab shows what you owe for a period, line by line, before you pay.
- **Archive** removes the partner from lists and distribution while keeping its name on past leads. Archived partners can be restored or, if you're sure, deleted permanently.
