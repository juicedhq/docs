A bid tells your provider: "Send me this type of lead from this place, and here's what I'll pay." Each bid covers one lead type in one location (a county, a state, or a whole country) and competes with other buyers' bids for leads that come in there. The Bids page is where you create, adjust, pause, and remove them.

## The bids table

Tabs across the top sort your bids by whether they can win leads, with a count on each:

- **All**: every bid you have.
- **Active**: bids that can win leads right now.
- **Limited**: bids that are still competing but are being held back by your wallet balance or a budget.
- **Inactive**: bids that can't win leads right now, because they're paused, out of funds, or have hit a cap or budget (on the bid or on your whole account).

Use **Search bids** and the **Filters** menu (**Lead type** and **Scope**) to narrow the list.

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Bid** | The location, with its state (or "Statewide" / "Nationwide") underneath |
| **My Bid** | Your bid amount. If your provider gives your return tier a discount, the discounted price shows in green next to your struck-through bid; hover it for the details. |
| **Top** | The highest bid for that lead type and location |
| **Position** | Where your bid stands. See [Bid position](#bid-position). |
| **Lead Type** | The lead type. Hover it for a description, if your provider wrote one. |
| **Status** | Whether the bid can win leads. See [Bid statuses](#bid-statuses). |

Use the **Columns** menu to show extra columns: **County**, **State**, and **Country** (handy for sorting), plus **Caps** and **Budgets** when your provider lets you set limits on individual bids.

Each row has three actions at the end:

- **Pause bid** / **Resume bid** stops or restarts that one bid without deleting it.
- **Edit bid** opens the bid editor for that location.
- **Delete bid** removes the bid after you confirm. You stop receiving leads for it immediately, and it can't be undone.

<Note>
If your provider has locked bidding, the pause and resume buttons are disabled. Contact your provider if you need a bid paused.
</Note>

## Bid position

| Position | What it means |
|----------|---------------|
| **Top** | You're the highest bidder for that lead type and location |
| **Tied** | You're tied with one or more bidders at the top amount. Leads are shared among the tied bidders. |
| **Under** | Someone is bidding more than you |
| **Out** | Your bid isn't competing right now, usually because it's paused or out of funds |

Being on top only wins you the lead if your bid can actually take it, so keep an eye on your status too.

## Bid statuses

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Active** | Competing for leads normally |
| **Limited** | Still competing, but your wallet balance or a remaining budget is lower than the bid amount, so the bid competes at that lower amount. Add funds or raise the budget to get back to full strength. |
| **Exhausted** | Out of service until something changes: your wallet can't cover the minimum, or a cap or budget has been used up for the period |
| **Paused** | Paused by you or your provider |
| **Archived** | No longer in use |

The [wallet chip](/customer-portal/getting-around#wallet) in the sidebar gives you the same funding signal at a glance.

## Creating a new bid

Click **New bid** at the top of the page.

### 1. Find a location

Start typing in the **Location** box (a county, state, or country). Matching places appear as you type. Use the **Scopes** checkboxes to show only counties, states, or countries.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/portal/bids/bid-modal-search-light.webp" alt="Location search results for Los Angeles in the New bid dialog" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/portal/bids/bid-modal-search-dark.webp" alt="Location search results for Los Angeles in the New bid dialog" />
</Frame>

Click a result to choose it. Prefer to browse? The [Locations](/customer-portal/locations) pages let you compare top bids across areas and start a bid from there.

### 2. Set your price

Once you've picked a location, you'll see every lead type you can bid on there.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/portal/bids/bid-modal-location-light.webp" alt="New bid dialog with a location selected, listing lead types with minimum and top bids and a button to bid the minimum" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/portal/bids/bid-modal-location-dark.webp" alt="New bid dialog with a location selected, listing lead types with minimum and top bids and a button to bid the minimum" />
</Frame>

Each lead type shows its **Min** (the lowest allowed bid) and **Top** (the current highest bid, with the number of bids tied there). To bid:

1. Click **Bid $X** to start at the minimum.
2. Use the left and right arrows to step your bid down or up, or click the amount to pick from a list of every allowed amount.
3. Watch the badge next to the amount: **TOP**, **TIED**, or **UNDER** tells you where that amount would land you before you commit.

Some lead types have a single fixed price, so there's only one amount to choose.

Next to the amount you'll find:

- **Limits** (when your provider allows them): set a daily, weekly, or monthly lead cap or budget for just this bid. The bid pauses itself once a limit is reached. Leave a field blank for no limit.
- **Clear bid** (the **×**): remove that lead type from what you're about to save.

You can bid on as many lead types at the location as you like. If you already have a bid for a lead type there, you'll see "You already have a bid for this lead type and location" instead; use **Edit bid** to change it.

### 3. Save

Click **Save bid** (or **Save N bids** if you set several). You're back on the bids table, and your new bids start competing right away.

## Editing a bid

Click **Edit bid** on any row. The **Edit bids** dialog opens on that bid's location (which can't be changed while editing) with all its lead types listed. Adjust prices or limits, add bids for other lead types, then click **Save changes**.

To stop bidding on a lead type entirely, use **Delete bid** on its row in the table.

## Smart pricing

You don't always pay your full bid. When you win a lead, Juiced charges you the lowest allowed amount that still beats the next-highest bidder, so you pay just enough to win.

**Example:** The minimum bid is &#36;10 and bids go up in &#36;10 steps. You bid &#36;100 and the next-highest bidder is at &#36;10. When you win, you pay &#36;20, not &#36;100.

So bid what a lead is genuinely worth to you. Competition sets the price; your bid just sets the ceiling.
