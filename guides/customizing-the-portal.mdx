title: Customizing the Customer Portal
description: Put your brand on the customer portal with your colors, logos, and favicon, plus support links, sign-up text, and footer controls.
---

The customer portal starts out with Juiced defaults, but nearly everything a customer sees can carry your brand. This guide walks through the settings that make the portal look and read like your own product. For field-by-field detail, see [Branding](/features/branding), [Company Profile](/features/company-profile), and [Registration & Login](/features/registration-and-login).

## Prerequisites

- The **Admin** [role](/features/roles).
- Your logo as a wide wordmark (plus a light version for dark mode, if your logo is dark), and a square icon. PNG, SVG, or JPG, up to 4 MB each.
- Your brand colors as hex codes.
- A support email address, a helpdesk URL, or both.

## 1. Open Branding

Click the gear in the rail and choose **Branding** under **Account**. Changes save together with the floating save bar, and the **Preview** at the bottom of the page updates as you go.

## 2. Set your colors

1. **Brand Color** is used for buttons, links, and highlights. It shows as text on both light and dark backgrounds, so pick something saturated, because pastels wash out.
2. **Accent Color** (optional) is a second brand color. Leave it empty to use the brand color.
3. **Rail Style** sets the background of the portal's narrow left rail. Pick **None**, **Primary**, **Accent**, or **Primary to Accent** for a gradient. With the gradient, **Reverse Direction** flips it.

Type a hex value, use the swatch, or click **Pick from screen** to sample a color from anything on your display.

## 3. Upload your logos

| Slot | Where it shows |
|------|----------------|
| **Primary Logo** | The portal navigation and the sign-in, sign-up, and password reset pages. A wide wordmark works best. |
| **Dark Mode Logo** | Used instead of the primary logo when a customer is in dark mode. Without one, the primary logo is used in dark mode too. |

<Tip>
If your logo is dark text, upload a light version as the **Dark Mode Logo**, or it'll be hard to see on a dark background. Switch your own panel to dark mode to check it in the preview.
</Tip>

## 4. Upload your icons

| Slot | Where it shows |
|------|----------------|
| **Favicon** | Browser tabs. Use a square image. |
| **Avatar** | Your company avatar in notifications, shown in a circle. Use a square image. |

Most tenants use the same square mark for both.

## 5. Hide the "Powered by Juiced" footer (if eligible)

On plans of $1,000/month or more, turn on **Hide "Powered by Juiced" Footer**. On other plans the switch is disabled. See [Billing](/features/billing#premium-features) for which plans include it.

Save with the floating save bar, then refresh the portal to see the new look.

## 6. Set your support links

Customers need a way to reach you. Click **Company Profile** under **Account** and fill in **Support**.

- **Support Email** is shown in the portal sidebar as **Email Us**.
- **Support Portal URL** is your helpdesk link, shown as **Support Portal**. To enter one, set **Primary Contact Method** to **URL**.

The portal shows a link for each one you've filled in. While you're here, check your company **Name**, which customers see throughout the portal.

## 7. Brand the sign-up page

Click **Registration & Login** under **Application** to find these settings.

- **Account Creation Headline** is the main call to action on the sign-up form.
- **Campaign Banner Text** is optional campaign text. When it's set, it becomes the page's title.
- **Consent Links** are your terms and privacy policy, which customers must accept to register. Turn on **Require consent links on login** to have them accepted at every login too.

## What's next

- **Your own domain** lets you serve the portal from `leads.yourcompany.com` instead of a Juiced subdomain. See [Adding a custom domain](/guides/adding-a-custom-domain).
- **Emails** already show your logo and colors. Make the words match too with [Customizing notification templates](/guides/customizing-notification-templates).
- **Banners** announce promotions and maintenance at the top of the portal. See [Banners](/features/banners).
