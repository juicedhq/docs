Integrations connect Juiced to the outside services that send your emails and texts, take your customers' payments, track your portal, and enrich your leads.

Open **Integrations** under **Integrations** in the sidebar. Each service has a card with its status: **Enabled**, **Not Configured**, or **Failing** when the service has started rejecting your credentials.

Click a card to connect it. Fill in the credentials and click **Connect**. Once it's connected, the same card opens the edit page: change what you need and click **Save changes** on the save bar, or click **Delete** in the header to disconnect it.

## Stripe

Stripe takes your customers' card payments: deposits, auto recharge, and subscriptions. Stripe is optional. If you don't collect payments from customers through Juiced (say, you bill them elsewhere, or you only sell to partners), you can skip it.

You don't connect Stripe from this page. Go to **Settings → Application → Payments & Deposits** and click **Connect Stripe** to go through Stripe's onboarding (see [Payments and deposits](/features/payments-and-deposits#connecting-stripe)). Once you're connected, the Stripe card shows **Enabled**, and its page shows your **Account ID**. It's managed automatically, so you can't edit or delete it here.

Without Stripe, you can still create customers and adjust their balances by hand. Customers can't add cards, make deposits, use auto recharge, or start card-based subscriptions until you connect it.

## SendGrid

SendGrid sends your customer notification emails. Connecting it turns on the **Email** channel in [notification templates](/features/notifications#channels).

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Key Name** | Optional. A name to help you recognize the API key. |
| **API Key ID** | The ID of your SendGrid API key. |
| **API Key** | The key itself. |
| **From Address** | The email address your notifications come from. |
| **From Name** | The sender name customers see. |

If SendGrid starts rejecting your key, the card shows **Failing** and the edit page explains what SendGrid said. Emails aren't sent until it's fixed, so check the account's credits and the key's permissions, then save a working key.

## Twilio

Twilio sends your customer notification text messages. Connecting it turns on the **SMS** channel in [notification templates](/features/notifications#channels). Enter your **Account SID**, **Auth Token**, and **Messaging Service SID**.

## Google Tag Manager

Add your Google Tag Manager **Container ID** to load your container in the customer portal and on its sign-in and sign-up pages. From there you can add analytics, conversion tracking, and marketing tags without touching code.

## Intercom

Add your Intercom **App ID** to show the Intercom messenger in your customer portal, so customers can reach your support team without leaving it.

## Real Estate API

The **RealEstateAPI** integration enriches leads with property and owner data from RealEstateAPI.com. It's especially handy for real estate leads, where property details help qualify and route them.

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Property Data API Key** | Your RealEstateAPI key, from the RealEstateAPI.com dashboard. |
| **Skip Trace API Key** | Optional. Needed for owner contact lookups, and it may be a separate key from your property data key. |

Both keys are encrypted. When editing, leave a key blank to keep the current one.

### Enriching leads during distribution

Enrichment runs as a **Real Estate API** stage in your [distribution stages](/features/distribution). The method appears once the integration is connected, and you can have one Real Estate API stage per lead category. In the stage's **Real Estate API Configuration**:

- **Lead Category**: only leads in this category are enriched.
- **Property Data Enrichment**: pick property values such as **Estimated Value**, **Bedrooms**, **Year Built**, **Owner 1 Full Name**, or **Last Sale Price**.
- **Skip Trace Owner Contacts**: pick owner values such as **Owner Names**, **Owner Phones**, and **Owner Emails**. This needs the Skip Trace API key.
- **Field Mappings**: map each picked value to one of your lead fields. Turn on **Overwrite Existing** for a field that should replace a value the lead already has; otherwise, enrichment only fills empty fields.

The stage doesn't end distribution: the lead moves on to the next stage with its new data, so later stages can use it in their conditions. The stage's result in the lead's [incoming log](/features/logs#incoming-logs) shows how it went, such as "No fields enriched" when the lookup found nothing to add. For setup steps, see [Configuring distribution stages](/guides/configuring-distribution-stages#real-estate-api).

<Warning>
Deleting the RealEstateAPI integration also deletes your Real Estate API distribution stages. Juiced tells you how many before you confirm.
</Warning>
