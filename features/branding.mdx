Make the customer portal feel like your own. Branding lets you customize the colors, logos, and icons your customers see when they sign in and use the platform. Think of it as the label on the bottle: the juice is the same, but now it's yours.

To configure your branding, click the gear in the rail and choose **Branding** under **Account**. Save your changes with the floating save bar.

## Colors

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Brand Color** | Used for buttons, links, and highlights throughout the customer portal, including the sign-in page and navigation. |
| **Accent Color** | A second color for your brand. If you don't set one, the brand color is used. |
| **Rail Style** | The background of the customer portal's navigation rail: **None**, **Primary**, **Accent**, or **Primary to Accent** (a gradient). |
| **Reverse Direction** | Only shown for **Primary to Accent**. Flips the gradient to run from accent to primary. |

Type a hex value, use the color swatch, or click **Pick from screen** to grab a color from anything on your display. Bold, saturated colors work best, since the brand color appears as text on both light and dark backgrounds.

## Logos & Icons

Drop an image onto a slot, or click it to browse. PNG, SVG, or JPG files up to 4 MB are accepted. Hover the info icon under each slot for a reminder of where it's used.

| Slot | Where it appears |
|------|------------------|
| **Primary Logo** | The customer portal navigation and the sign-in, sign-up, and password reset pages. A horizontally rectangular logo works best. |
| **Dark Mode Logo** | Shown instead of the primary logo when a customer uses dark mode. |
| **Favicon** | Browser tabs. Use a square image. |
| **Avatar** | Your company avatar in notifications. Use a square image; it's shown in a circle. |

Most tenants use the same image for the favicon and avatar.

<Tip>
If your primary logo is dark text, upload a light version as the **Dark Mode Logo** so it doesn't disappear on a dark background. Until you do, the preview falls back to your primary logo.
</Tip>

## Footer

By default, the customer portal footer shows "Powered by Juiced". On plans of **$1,000/month or more** ([Berry Boost and up](/features/billing#premium-features)), you can turn on **Hide "Powered by Juiced" Footer** to remove it.

On lower plans, the switch is disabled with the note "Upgrade your plan to enable this feature." Juiced can also enable it for your account by arrangement.
