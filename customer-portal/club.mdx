Some providers run a Club: a members-only pool of leads you can claim for free as part of a monthly subscription. Claimed leads land in your usual [Leads](/customer-portal/leads) list and flow through your normal [webhooks](/customer-portal/webhooks) and notifications, just like leads you won.

Your provider names their Club, so in your sidebar it might say "Fresh Squeeze Club" or something else entirely. This page calls it the Club. You'll only see it if your provider runs one.

## Joining the Club

If you don't have access yet, the Club page shows your provider's pitch, the plan name, and the starting monthly price.

Click **Get started** to choose a plan and subscribe on the [Subscription](/customer-portal/subscription) page. Your access starts as soon as the subscription is active.

If you already have a subscription but it isn't giving you access (for example, the first payment didn't go through), you'll see "Your subscription needs attention" with a **Manage subscription** button instead. Sort out the payment there and the Club opens back up.

Your provider can also give you Club access directly, without a subscription.

## Finding a lead

Once you're in, the Club page lists the leads you can claim.

Each row shows the lead's **County** (with its state), **Lead type**, and when it was **Received**. Sort by any of them, or click **Filters** to narrow by **State**, **County**, or **Lead type** (pick as many as you like). Only leads that can still be claimed are listed.

## Claiming a lead

Click **Claim**, then confirm in the **Claim this lead?** dialog.

- Claiming is free. Nothing comes out of your wallet.
- The lead is delivered to your [Leads](/customer-portal/leads) list with the **Club** status, and to your CRM if you have a webhook set up.
- You can claim each lead only once.
- Other members may be able to claim the same lead too, depending on how your provider runs the Club.

<Note>
A Club lead isn't an exclusive lead. If you need to know how many other buyers might receive it, ask your provider before you work it.
</Note>

### Your claim allowance

Some plans include a set number of claims per billing period. If yours does, the top of the table shows how many you have left, for example **8 of 10 claims left**. When you reach zero, the **Claim** buttons are disabled until your next billing period starts. Plans without a limit don't show a counter.

## Managing your subscription

Everything about your plan (subscribing, promotion codes, canceling, resuming, and fixing a failed payment) lives on the [Subscription](/customer-portal/subscription) page under **Billing**.
