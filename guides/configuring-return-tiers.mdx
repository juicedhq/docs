---
title: Configuring Return Tiers
description: Turn on return tiers, build a ladder that rewards low returners and penalizes heavy ones, and recalculate every customer in one click.
---

Returns keep customers honest about bad leads, but a return policy without teeth gets abused. **Return tiers** group customers by their return rate and attach a reward or penalty to each group. This guide sets up a tier ladder from scratch. For how each setting behaves, see [Returns](/features/returns).

## Prerequisites

- The **Admin** [role](/features/roles).
- A policy in mind: which return rates are fine, which deserve a nudge, and whether to reward good behavior with discounts.
- Optionally, a page explaining your return policy, so customers can read the rules.

## Step 1: Open the Returns page

Click the gear in the rail and choose **Returns** under **Application**. The page has **Return Settings** at the top and the **Return Tiers** table below.

## Step 2: Set up returns

1. Turn on **Enable return tiers**. Customers are then assigned to a tier automatically based on their return rate.
2. Set the **Lead volume lookback**: the assigned lead volume, in dollars, that return rates are calculated over. It defaults to $5,000. Customers who haven't reached it yet count as new customers.
3. Add your policy page as the **Lead Returns URL**, so customers can find it from their portal.
4. Leave **Hide return tiers from customers** off for now. You can't offer a discount while tiers are hidden, because customers have to be able to see price changes.
5. Save with the floating save bar.

## Step 3: Create your first tier

Click **New tier**.

**Who it's for and how it looks**

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Name** | Shown on the customer's badge, like "Golden" or "Overripe" |
| **Headline**, **Brief description**, **Long description** | What the tier means, in increasing detail. Spell out the rules and any penalty here so customers understand it. |
| **Color** and **Icon** | The badge's look in the portal |

**Behavior**

- **Lower bound (%)** and **Upper bound (%)**: the return-rate range for this tier. Ranges can't overlap; Juiced tells you if one does.
- **Apply to new customers**: makes this the tier for customers still below the lookback volume, in place of bounds.
- **Add a discount** with a **Discount percentage**: customers in this tier pay less when they win, while their bid still competes at full price.
- **Add a penalty** with a **Penalty percentage**, or **Match return rate** to make the penalty equal the customer's own return rate.

<Warning>
Penalties are easy to misread. A customer with a 20% penalty bidding $100 competes as if they bid $80, so they win less often, but when they do win they pay the full $100. Explain it in the tier's long description, or expect "I never win anymore" emails.
</Warning>

Click **Create**.

## Step 4: Build out the ladder

Repeat Step 3 for each bracket. A typical pattern:

| Tier | Bracket | Effect |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Fresh** | New customers | None, while they build history |
| **Golden** | 0 to 5% | 5% discount |
| **Ripe** | 5 to 15% | None |
| **Overripe** | 15 to 25% | 15% penalty |
| **Bruised** | 25 to 100% | Match return rate |

Three to five tiers is plenty; past that, customers stop reading the ladder. Cover 0 to 100% without gaps, or a customer whose rate falls in a gap gets no tier.

Use **Move up** and **Move down** to set the order customers see the tiers in. Order doesn't affect which tier a customer lands in; their return rate does. Most-favorable first is the usual choice.

## Step 5: Recalculate

Click **Recalculate** in the page header and confirm. Juiced goes through every customer and assigns the tier that matches their current return rate. Customers whose tier changes get the **Return Tier Updated** notification.

After that, tiers keep themselves up to date as customers are assigned leads and their returns are approved. Run **Recalculate** again whenever you change the tiers.

<Tip>
If you're about to make several tier changes in a row, consider turning off the **Return Tier Updated** template in [Notification Templates](/features/notifications) first, so customers don't get an email for every tweak. Turn it back on before the final recalculation.
</Tip>

## Step 6: Exclude a customer (optional)

For a customer who shouldn't be tiered, such as a strategic account with its own contract or an internal test account, open the customer, click **Edit**, and turn on **Disable Return Tiers** under **Toggles**. They're left out of tier calculations, discounts, and penalties, and their return rate shows instead of a tier.

## What customers see

Unless you hide tiers, customers see their tier as a badge in the portal and can open it to compare every tier. If you'd rather they didn't:

- **Hide return tiers from customers** shows their return percentage instead of the badge. Penalties still apply.
- With tiers turned off, the **Return rate message** is shown instead (use `:rate` and `:lookback` in it), or turn on **Hide return percentages from customers** to remove return rates from the portal entirely.

See [Returns in the customer portal](/customer-portal/returns) for their side of things.
