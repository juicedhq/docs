The Customer Portal is where you buy leads from your lead provider. You reach it at your provider's `/app` address. It's completely separate from the system your provider uses to run their side of the operation, so everything here is about your account, meaning your bids, your leads, and your wallet.

Your provider brands the portal, so their name, colors, and icon appear throughout. If you buy for more than one business, each one is its own account with its own wallet, bids, and leads. [Getting around the portal](/customer-portal/getting-around) shows you how to switch between them.

## Portal sections

| Section | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **[Dashboard](/customer-portal/dashboard)** | Your latest leads and recent bids at a glance |
| **[Leads](/customer-portal/leads)** | Search, filter, and export the leads you've received |
| **[Bids](/customer-portal/bids)** | Create and manage the bids that win you leads |
| **[Marketplace](/customer-portal/marketplace)** | Buy leads directly from live auctions (when your provider offers it) |
| **[Club](/customer-portal/club)** | Claim leads included with a subscription (when your provider offers it; the name varies) |
| **[Locations](/customer-portal/locations)** | Compare top bids by county, state, or country before you bid |
| **[Notifications](/customer-portal/notifications)** | Choose who gets emails and texts about leads, bids, and your account |
| **[Webhooks](/customer-portal/webhooks)** | Send each new lead straight to your CRM or another system |
| **[Billing](/customer-portal/billing)** | Payment methods, transactions, and auto recharge |
| **[Subscription](/customer-portal/subscription)** | Start or manage a subscription (when your provider offers one) |
| **[Bidding Limits](/customer-portal/bidding-limits)** | Pause your account and cap your leads and spending |
| **Support** | Links to your provider's support portal, email, and lead returns form |

Not seeing one of these? Your provider decides which features are turned on for your account. [Why a page might be missing](/customer-portal/getting-around#why-a-page-might-be-missing) explains each one.

Your personal login settings live under **User Settings** in the menu at the bottom of the sidebar. See [Profile and security](/customer-portal/account-settings).

## Getting started

New to the portal? Here's the usual order of operations.

1. **[Create your account](/customer-portal/signing-up-and-logging-in)**. Set up your login, then your business details.
2. **[Fund your wallet](/customer-portal/billing#adding-funds)**. Leads are paid for from your wallet balance.
3. **[Place your first bids](/customer-portal/bids#creating-a-new-bid)**. Pick locations and lead types and set your price.
4. **[Get leads where you work](/customer-portal/notifications)**. Add notification contacts, or connect a [webhook](/customer-portal/webhooks) to your CRM.
5. **[Set your limits](/customer-portal/bidding-limits)**. Cap leads and spending so a busy day doesn't squeeze your budget dry.

Your provider may have already set up some of this when they created your account.
