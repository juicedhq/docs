---
title: Customizing Notification Templates
description: Rewrite the email, SMS, and in-app messages Juiced sends your customers, and decide which ones customers can turn off.
---

Every time something happens on a customer's account (a lead assigned, a bid outbid, a return approved), Juiced tells them by email, text, or in-app notification. The default copy works, but it sounds like Juiced. This guide makes it sound like you. For the full list of events and variables, see [Notifications](/features/notifications).

## Prerequisites

- The **Admin** [role](/features/roles).
- [SendGrid](/features/integrations#sendgrid) set up for email templates, and [Twilio](/features/integrations#twilio) for SMS. Without them, those channels don't appear.
- A sense of your voice (friendly, brisk, formal, whatever matches the rest of your customer communication).

## 1. Open Notification Templates

Click the gear in the rail and choose **Notification Templates** under **Application**.

  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/settings/notification-templates-dark.webp" alt="Notification Templates list with one row per notification type and Visible and Default columns for each channel" />

Each row is a notification type with a short description of when it's sent. For each channel (**Email**, **SMS**, **In-App**), two columns show its settings.

- **Visible** means customers can see and change this notification in their preferences.
- **Default** means it's turned on for new contacts.

A type that doesn't support a channel shows a dash there. Payment emails, for example, are email-only.

## 2. Open a template

Click a notification type. The top of the page lists its **Available variables**, and below that is a card per channel (**Email Template**, **SMS Template**, and **In-App Template**).

## 3. Write the copy

Each card has two fields.

- **Subject** is the email subject, or the in-app notification's title. SMS has no subject.
- **Body** is the message. Drop in variables from the list at the top, for example `{{ lead.full_name }}` or `{{ customer.name }}`.

A Lead Assigned email might use the subject `New {{ lead.leadType.name }} lead in {{ lead.city }}` and this body.

```text
Hi {{ customer.name }},
You just won a new lead: {{ lead.full_name }} in {{ lead.city }}.
Phone: {{ lead.phone }}
```

A few things to know about how messages come out.

- **Emails** keep basic formatting (bold, italic, underline, strikethrough), and each line becomes its own paragraph. Lines written as `Label: value`, like the phone line above, are laid out as a tidy details list.
- **Emails get a button** to the relevant page automatically, so you don't need to paste the link in.
- **SMS** should be short. Put the important detail first.

<Warning>
Only use variables listed for that notification type. Juiced checks when you save, and a variable that doesn't exist for this type (say, a lead field on Customer Paused, which has no lead) shows an error.
</Warning>

## 4. Decide who can turn it off

Each card also has two switches.

- **Visible to customers** hides the notification from customers' preferences when it's off, so they can't change it.
- **Enabled by default** sets whether new contacts start with it on.

They work together. Visible and on by default is the usual setup. Hidden and on makes a must-send message. Visible and off makes it opt-in, which suits chatty notifications like Bid Matched.

## 5. Save

Click **Save all** in the floating save bar. Every channel saves together.

If you get it wrong, **Reset to Default** on a card restores that channel's default template, and **Reset All to Default** at the top restores every channel for this type. Both ask you to confirm, and there's no undo, so copy anything you want to keep first.

## 6. Test it

Add yourself as a [contact](/features/customers#contacts) on a test customer, then use the contact's **Test** action to send a test notification. Or wait for the real thing by posting a test lead through one of your sources and watching it arrive.

## Good to know

- Branding carries through, so your [logo and colors](/features/branding) appear in emails without extra setup.
- Customers manage which notifications each contact gets under **Notifications → Contacts** in their portal. See [Notifications in the customer portal](/customer-portal/notifications).
- In-app notifications land in the bell of every user on the customer's account.
- Sending lead data to a CRM is a different job. Use [webhooks](/features/webhooks) for that.
