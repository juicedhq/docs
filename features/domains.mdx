The **Domains & Links** page controls how people reach you: whether visitors can browse your marketplace without an account, where your customer-facing links send people who aren't signed in, and the domain your customer portal lives on. Find it under **Settings → Account → Domains & Links**.

## Public Marketplace

Turn on **Enable public marketplace** to let visitors who aren't signed in browse your available leads at `/marketplace`. It's a shop window: prospects can see what you sell before they create an account. See [Marketplace](/features/marketplace) for how the marketplace itself works.

## Customer Portal Entry Point

**Unauthenticated visitors see** decides where your customer-facing links send people who aren't signed in:

- **Customer sign-in page**: the default.
- **Marketplace**: only offered when your distribution plan has a marketplace stage. If the public marketplace is off, links always go to the sign-in page.

Save the marketplace and entry point settings with the floating save bar.

## Custom domain

By default, customers reach your portal through a Juiced subdomain (like `yourcompany.usejuiced.com/app`). With a custom domain, they reach it through your own domain instead, for example `leads.yourcompany.com/app`.

### Who can use a custom domain

Custom domains are a [premium feature](/features/billing#premium-features) on plans of **$500/month or more** (Citrus Splash and up). Juiced can also enable it for your account. Without access, the page shows a **Custom Domains: Higher Plan Required** card with an **Upgrade your plan** link instead of the domain field. If someone visits your custom domain while your plan doesn't include it, they're redirected to your standard subdomain.

### Setting it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your domain">
    In the **Custom domain** field, enter a subdomain on a domain you own, like `leads.yourcompany.com`. Don't include `https://`. Click **Update custom domain** and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the DNS records">
    Juiced shows the DNS records to add at your registrar under **Domain Validation**. Each card lists the record's **Type** (CNAME), **Name**, and **Value**: one points your domain at Juiced, and the other proves you own it. Copy them exactly.

    DNS changes can take up to 24 hours to propagate, though most are much faster.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check validation">
    Each record shows a status: **Validated**, **Pending**, or **Incomplete**. Click **Check again** to refresh. Once everything is validated, Juiced issues an SSL certificate automatically, and the page shows "Your domain ... has been verified and is now active."
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Changing your domain

You can change your custom domain at most once per hour. Customers lose access through the old domain once the new one validates, and the new domain must be validated before it works.

Once the SSL certificate has been issued, the field locks. To move to a different domain after that, contact support.

## What the domain applies to

Your custom domain covers the **customer portal**: the `/app` interface where your customers sign in, view leads, manage their account, and visit the marketplace.

```text
https://{your-custom-domain}/app
```

Once you've added a custom domain, links to your [checkout pages](/features/checkout-pages) use it too. Connecting [AI assistants](/features/ai-assistants) requires a verified custom domain.

## Requirements

**Domain format:**
- Must be a valid domain without a scheme (`leads.example.com`, not `https://leads.example.com`)
- Use a subdomain on a domain you own (e.g., `app.yourcompany.com`, `leads.yourcompany.com`)

**SSL/TLS:**
- SSL certificates are provisioned automatically; you don't need to provide your own
- Certificates are managed and renewed automatically

## Troubleshooting

**A record is stuck on Pending**

DNS hasn't propagated yet, or a record wasn't added exactly as shown. Double-check each record at your registrar, then click **Check again**. You can use a tool like [dnschecker.org](https://dnschecker.org) to check propagation.

**SSL certificate not issuing**

This usually resolves itself once DNS propagates. If it's been more than 24 hours, verify the CNAME records are correct and that there are no conflicting records (like an existing A record for the same subdomain).
