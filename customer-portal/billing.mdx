Leads are paid for from your **wallet**, a prepaid balance on your account. The **Billing** group in the sidebar holds everything that keeps it full, including **Payment methods**, **Transactions**, and **Auto recharge**, plus [Subscription](/customer-portal/subscription) and [Bidding Limits](/customer-portal/bidding-limits) when they apply to you. Your current balance is always on the [wallet chip](/customer-portal/getting-around#wallet) at the top of the sidebar.

<Note>
If your provider doesn't take card payments, you won't see **Payment methods** or **Auto recharge**, and your provider adds funds to your wallet for you. You can still review everything in **Transactions**.
</Note>

### Adding a card

Click **Add card**. You're taken to a secure Stripe page to enter your card details, then brought back to the portal with the card saved.

### Managing cards

Each card has three actions.

- **Add funds** charges that card and tops up your wallet (see below).
- **Make default** makes it the card [auto recharge](#auto-recharge) uses.
- **Remove card** deletes it after you confirm. You can't remove your default card (make another card the default first) or the card your subscription bills (cancel the subscription first).

## Adding funds

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/portal/billing/add-funds-light.webp" alt="Add funds dialog charging a Mastercard, with an Amount field, a 3% manual deposit fee note, the total to be charged, and a tip to reduce the fee to 1% with auto-recharge" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/portal/billing/add-funds-dark.webp" alt="Add funds dialog charging a Mastercard, with an Amount field, a 3% manual deposit fee note, the total to be charged, and a tip to reduce the fee to 1% with auto-recharge" />
</Frame>

1. Click **Add funds** on the card you want to charge.
2. Enter the **Amount** to add to your wallet. Your provider may set a minimum, and the minimum for your very first deposit can be different.
3. Check the fee box. It shows your provider's **Manual deposit fee** (if any) and exactly what **You will be charged**. If auto recharge has a lower fee, it tells you how much you'd save. Some providers waive the fee on your first deposit.
4. Click **Charge**.

You'll see "Funds added! Your wallet balance may take a moment to update." The new balance shows on the wallet chip shortly after.

<Note>
If a yellow banner says card payments aren't available for your account, your provider has turned them off. Contact them to add funds.
</Note>

## Transactions

Transactions is your wallet's complete history, covering every deposit, purchase, refund, and adjustment.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/portal/billing/transactions-light.webp" alt="Transactions table with date, signed amount, event, and transaction ID columns and a running total below" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/portal/billing/transactions-dark.webp" alt="Transactions table with date, signed amount, event, and transaction ID columns and a running total below" />
</Frame>

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Date (UTC)** | When it happened, in UTC |
| **Amount** | Green with **+** for money in, red with **−** for money out |
| **Event** | What happened (see below), with a link to the related lead or record when there is one |
| **Transaction ID** | A unique ID to quote if you ask your provider about a charge |

Search by transaction ID, or use **Filters** to narrow by **Type** (**Deposit** or **Withdraw**), **Event**, and **Date**. Under the table, **Total** adds up every transaction matching your filters. Filter to **Lead Assigned** for last month, for example, and you have your lead spend in one number.

### Event types

| Event | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Funds Added** | You added funds with a card |
| **Auto Recharge** | Auto recharge topped up your wallet |
| **Funds Added By Tenant** | Your provider added funds to your wallet |
| **Funds Subtracted By Tenant** | Your provider removed funds from your wallet |
| **Lead Assigned** | You were charged for a lead |
| **Lead Returned** | A returned lead was refunded |
| **Placed Buy Order** | Funds set aside for a [marketplace buy order](/customer-portal/marketplace#placing-a-buy-order) |
| **Canceled Buy Order** | A buy order you canceled was refunded |
| **Released Buy Order** | A buy order's funds came back when its auction ended |
| **Checkout Bonus** | Bonus credit from one of your provider's offers |

To see a single transaction's full record, search for its ID with [Search everything](/customer-portal/getting-around#search-everything) (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>).

## Auto recharge

Auto recharge keeps your wallet topped up so your bids never run dry mid-day. When your balance drops below an amount you choose, Juiced charges your default card and adds funds.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/portal/billing/auto-recharge-light.webp" alt="Auto recharge settings with an Enable auto recharge switch turned off" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/portal/billing/auto-recharge-dark.webp" alt="Auto recharge settings with an Enable auto recharge switch turned off" />
</Frame>

1. Turn on **Enable auto recharge**.
2. Set **When my wallet falls below** to the balance that triggers a recharge.
3. Set **Add the following funds** to the amount to add each time. Your provider may set a minimum, shown under the field.
4. Save your changes.

For example, with a trigger of &#36;100 and an amount of &#36;500, your card is charged &#36;500 whenever your balance dips below &#36;100.

If your balance is already below the trigger when you turn auto recharge on, a recharge runs straight away. Any auto recharge fee is shown before you save, along with what each recharge will cost. Many providers charge less for auto recharge than for manual deposits.

### When auto recharge is unavailable

If the settings are disabled, a notice explains why with one of these messages.

- **You need an active payment method.** Add a card on [Payment methods](#payment-methods).
- **Your payment method has expired.** Add a new card and make it your default.
- **Card payments aren't available for your account.** Your provider has turned off card payments.
- **Your account has been archived.** Contact your provider.
