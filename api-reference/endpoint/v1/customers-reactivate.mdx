---
title: Reactivate a Customer
description: Bring an archived customer back.
openapi: POST /v1/customers/{id}/reactivate
---

Reactivating works just like reactivating a customer in Juiced. Their bids come back paused, so they won't receive leads until the bids are turned back on.

If another active customer now uses the archived customer's email, reactivating is refused. Change that customer's email first.

Reactivating a customer who isn't archived changes nothing and returns them, so it's safe to retry.
