## Customer portal refresh

The customer portal has a refreshed interface for the day-to-day work of buying and receiving leads:

- Search, filter, sort, and export purchased leads from the **Leads** page.
- Buy multiple available marketplace leads in one confirmed purchase.
- Configure webhooks with authentication, headers, request settings, test deliveries, delivery history, and re-delivery controls.

## Customer management improvements

- Sort customers by wallet balance and filter the customer list by a minimum or maximum balance.
- Choose from additional supported billing countries when registering a new Juiced tenant. State or province selection remains structured for US, Canadian, and Australian registrations.

## Fixes

- Fixed branding uploads on tenant custom domains and subdomains.
- Improved handling of duplicate custom-domain updates.
- Improved Safari rendering for maps on customer lead details.
- Prevented duplicate workflow webhooks for the same lead event.
