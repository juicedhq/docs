---
title: Get a Lead
description: Get one lead by ID, with its status, location, price and every customer or partner it was sold to.
openapi: GET /v1/leads/{id}
---

Use the lead's ID, which starts with `lead_`. It's the `id` from [Submit a Lead](/api-reference/endpoint/v1/leads), from [List Leads](/api-reference/endpoint/v1/leads-list), and from your [webhooks](/features/webhooks#public-ids). Test leads are returned too, with `test` set to `true`.

`assignments` has one entry for each sale, to a customer or a partner, including any that were returned. Exactly one of `customer` and `partner` is set on each entry, and `price` is what that buyer paid. A returned assignment has `returned_at` set, so a lead sold twice where one buyer returned it reads correctly. A partner send that's still in progress isn't listed until it's delivered. The lead's own `price` is what its first sale went for. See [List Leads](/api-reference/endpoint/v1/leads-list#statuses) for the `status` values.

No contact details are returned. Get those from [Get Lead Details](/api-reference/endpoint/v1/leads-details).
