Juiced has a handful of core nouns that show up everywhere. Once these click, the rest of the documentation reads a lot faster.

## The supply side

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sources" icon={<HugeIcon name="app-window" card />} href="/features/sources">
    The API endpoints where leads arrive. Each source belongs to a lead category and has its own credentials.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Leads" icon={<HugeIcon name="contact-book" card />} href="/features/leads">
    An individual prospect submitted to your platform. A lead is evaluated, priced, and routed to a buyer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lead categories" icon={<HugeIcon name="folder-tree" card />} href="/features/lead-categories">
    The schema for a kind of lead you sell, such as Auto Insurance, Solar, or Home Services. Defines the fields every lead in that category carries.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Lead types" icon={<HugeIcon name="tags" card />} href="/features/lead-types">
    Pricing tiers within a category. Conditions decide which leads qualify for which type; a fallback type catches everything else.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The selling side

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Distribution" icon={<HugeIcon name="left-to-right-list-number" card />} href="/features/distribution">
    The pipeline a lead flows through after it arrives. Composed of stages, and most tenants use Bidding and Marketplace.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bidding" icon={<HugeIcon name="hold-01" card />} href="/features/bidding">
    Real-time auctions where customers compete on price for matching leads. Each lead type has its own bidding rules.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Marketplace" icon={<HugeIcon name="store-01" card />} href="/features/marketplace">
    A fallback stage where unsold leads are offered at a fixed price to any eligible customer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ping-post" icon={<HugeIcon name="radar-01" card />} href="/features/ping-post">
    An alternate distribution pattern where partners respond to a "ping" with a bid before receiving the full lead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Club" icon={<HugeIcon name="ticket-01" card />} href="/features/club">
    A members-only pool of free leads that customers claim through a paid subscription or access you grant them.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## The people

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Customers" icon={<HugeIcon name="user-multiple" card />} href="/features/customers">
    The buyers who purchase leads from you. They fund a wallet, place bids, and receive leads in their own portal.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Partners" icon={<HugeIcon name="handshake" card />} href="/features/partners">
    External companies on either side of the trade. They can buy your leads over ping-post, supply leads through sources you assign them, or both. Partners sit outside the wallet model.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How it fits together

A lead arrives at a **source** → gets classified against the source's **lead category** and matched to a **lead type** → enters **distribution** → runs through stages like **bidding** and the **marketplace** → the winning **customer** or **partner** receives it, and the wallet or invoice is settled.

That's the whole loop. Everything else in Juiced (budgets and caps, duplicate detection, returns, notifications, webhooks) exists to make that loop run cleanly at scale.
