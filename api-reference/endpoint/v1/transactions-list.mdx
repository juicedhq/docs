---
title: List Transactions
description: List a customer's transactions, with their balance after each one.
openapi: GET /v1/customers/{id}/transactions
---

This lists every [transaction](/features/transactions) on the customer's balance, newest first, 50 at a time. Pass `next_cursor` back as `cursor` to get the next page. Archived customers' transactions can be listed too.

It needs the `customers:read` ability. There's no separate ability for reading transactions.

## Amounts and balances

- **`amount`** is signed, positive for a credit and negative for a debit.
- **`balance`** is the customer's balance right after that transaction, and it carries over correctly from page to page.
- **`lead`** is the lead's ID on transactions for a lead, such as a lead charge or a refund for a returned lead.
- **`receipt_url`** is only set on payments and credits that came with a receipt.

`balance` here doesn't subtract funds held for pending pings, while the customer's own `balance` from [Get a Customer](/api-reference/endpoint/v1/customers-get) does. So the newest transaction's `balance` can be higher than the customer's while a ping is waiting.

## Events

`event` says what produced each transaction.

| `event` | What happened |
|---------|---------------|
| `customer_payment` | The customer added funds in their portal, or bought lead credit on a [checkout page](/features/checkout-pages). |
| `auto_recharge` | The customer's card was charged automatically when their balance ran low. |
| `checkout_bonus` | Bonus credit that came with a checkout page purchase. |
| `tenant_credit` | You added funds, in Juiced or with [Add or Remove Funds](/api-reference/endpoint/v1/transactions-create). |
| `tenant_debit` | You removed funds, in Juiced or through the API. |
| `lead_charge` | The customer was charged for a lead. |
| `lead_refund` | A returned lead was refunded. |
| `buy_order_placed` | A marketplace buy order held funds. |
| `buy_order_canceled` | The customer canceled a buy order, releasing its funds. |
| `buy_order_released` | A buy order expired or lost, releasing its funds. |
