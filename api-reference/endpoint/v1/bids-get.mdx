---
title: Get a Bid
description: Get one bid by ID, with whether it can receive leads right now and why not.
openapi: GET /v1/bids/{id}
---

Use the bid's ID, which starts with `bid_`. Archived bids are returned too, with `archived_at` set. It needs the `bids:read` ability.

## Can it receive leads?

`status` is the bid's own status, as Juiced shows it on the bid. It doesn't look at the customer's account, so a bid can read `active` while its customer is paused.

`can_receive_leads` answers the real question. It runs the same check distribution does when it picks a buyer, so `true` means the bid can win leads right now. When it's `false`, `blocked_by` lists every reason, bid-level reasons first.

| `blocked_by` | What it means |
|--------------|---------------|
| `bid_archived` | The bid is archived. |
| `bid_paused` | The bid is paused. |
| `insufficient_funds` | The customer's balance can't cover the bid. |
| `bid_cap_reached` | The bid has hit one of its lead caps for the period. |
| `bid_budget_reached` | The bid has used up one of its budgets for the period. |
| `customer_paused` | The customer is paused. |
| `customer_archived` | The customer is archived. |
| `customer_cap_reached` | The customer has hit one of their account lead caps for the period. |
| `customer_budget_reached` | The customer's remaining account budget for the period can't cover the bid. |
| `bidding_not_set_up` | The lead type has no bidding settings at the bid's scope. |

`blocked_by` is empty whenever `can_receive_leads` is `true`. New codes may be added, so treat one you don't recognize as a reason the bid can't receive leads.

`below_minimum` doesn't block a bid. It flags an `amount` under the lead type's `minimum`, which is allowed.
