Customers, the businesses that buy your leads, can get into Juiced a few ways. They register themselves from your portal, you create the account and invite them, or they buy through a [checkout page](/features/checkout-pages). This guide covers each path and how to tune the experience. For everything on the customer record, see [Customers](/features/customers).

## Prerequisites

- The **Admin** or **Customer Support** [role](/features/roles). Both can create customers and send invites.
- An idea of how customers should arrive. Self-serve suits marketing-driven signups, while creating accounts yourself suits hand-sold deals and migrations.

## Self-serve registration

Anyone with your portal link can create an account. You just need to share it.

### 1. Share your registration link

Registration lives at `/app/register` on your Juiced subdomain, or on your [custom domain](/features/domains#custom-domain) once you have one, for example `https://leads.yourcompany.com/app/register`.

<Tip>
Add UTM parameters to see which campaigns drive signups, for example `?utm_source=google&utm_campaign=spring-launch`. The customer's overview shows the full **URL** and each parameter under **Registration Tracking**.
</Tip>

### 2. Customize the registration page

Click the gear in the rail and choose **Registration & Login** under **Application**.

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Campaign Banner Text** | Optional campaign text. When set, it becomes the sign-up page's title, with the headline beneath it. Handy for matching a specific ad campaign. |
| **Account Creation Headline** | The main call to action on the form. Defaults to "Create your user account". |
| **Consent Links** | Links (text and URL) customers must accept with a checkbox before registering. Use them for your terms, privacy policy, and similar. |
| **Require consent links on login** | Shows the same links on the login page, and customers must accept them each time they log in. |

Save with the floating save bar. See [Registration & Login](/features/registration-and-login) for the full page, and [Branding](/features/branding) for the logo and colors customers see.

### 3. What the customer does

Customers create their login, verify their email, and then fill in **Set up your business** with their business name, billing email, phone, and address (United States, Canada, or Australia). They land on their dashboard, ready to add funds. [Signing up and logging in](/customer-portal/signing-up-and-logging-in) covers their side step by step.

<Note>
Registration is always open. If you want invite-only access, don't publish the registration link.
</Note>

## Create the customer yourself

Use this for hand-sold accounts, migrations, or test accounts.

### 1. Create the customer

Click **Customers** in the sidebar, then **New customer**.

  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/customers/customer-create-dark.webp" alt="New customer form with Basic Info, Address, Toggles, and Auto Recharge sections" />
</Frame>

1. In **Basic Info**, enter the Name, Business Email, and Phone.
2. In **Address**, the street address, city, state, and ZIP are required. Country can be the United States, Canada, or Australia.
3. Under **Toggles**, **Can Access Bidding** starts off for customers you create. Turn it on if they should place their own bids.
4. **Bidding Limits** (only if you've turned on account-level limits) holds optional caps and budgets. See [Budgets and caps](/features/budgets-and-caps).
5. **Auto Recharge** is optional. See [Managing customer finances](/guides/managing-customer-finances#6-set-up-auto-recharge).
6. Click **Create customer**.

### 2. Invite their team

A customer you create has no users yet, and Juiced doesn't email anyone when you create one. Nobody can log in until you invite them.

1. Open the customer's **Users** tab and click **Invite user**.
2. Enter the person's **Email** and send it.

They get an email with a link to accept the invitation and create their login. People you invite join the customer as customer admins.

## Add someone to an existing customer

To add a teammate to an account that already exists, use the same **Users** tab. Click **Invite user**, enter their email, and send. If the email already belongs to someone on that customer, or already has an invite waiting, Juiced tells you instead of sending a duplicate.

Pending invites show in the list with two actions.

- **Resend invite** sends the email again, for example when it landed in spam
- **Revoke invite** cancels it, so the link stops working

## Checkout pages

If Juiced has enabled [checkout pages](/features/checkout-pages) for your account, a prospect can buy a credit package or subscription from a public page and get a customer account in the same step. Affiliate signups show up under **Registration Tracking** on the customer's overview.

## What happens next

However a customer arrives, keep these in mind.

- **Funding** starts at zero. They add funds from their portal, or you can **Add funds** from the customer's **More actions** menu. See [Managing customer finances](/guides/managing-customer-finances).
- **Bidding access** (**Can Access Bidding**) starts off for every new customer, however they arrive. Turn it on from the customer's **Edit** page. To grant it automatically, set **Minimum deposit for auto-access** under **Settings → Bidding**, and customers get access once they deposit that much.
- **Notifications** (emails and texts) go to the customer's contacts. Check who's set up on the customer's [Contacts](/features/customers#contacts) tab. Customers can add their own under **Notifications → Contacts** in the portal.
- For **Tracking**, check **Registration Tracking** on the customer's overview to see which link or campaign they came from.
