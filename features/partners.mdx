Partners are the businesses you trade leads with outside your own customer base. A partner can be on the supply side (affiliates and vendors whose [sources](/features/sources) send you leads), on the buy side (a lead buyer you ping for a price and post leads to), or both. Customers buy through your portal; partners plug in over the wire.

## Partners vs. sources

| Concept | What it represents | Example |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **Partner** | A business you trade leads with | "Acme Lead Gen Inc." |
| **Source** | A technical endpoint that receives leads | "Acme - Auto Insurance Form" |

A partner can have several sources, one for each form, campaign, or integration they run. The partner holds the business relationship; sources handle the technical details.

If your role can see analytics, the top of the page shows partner performance for the selected **Timeframe**. Hover the info icon on each stat for its exact definition.

| Stat | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Net Revenue** | What customers were charged for partner-sourced leads, net of discounts, excluding returned deliveries and free Club claims |
| **Payout** | What you owe partners for accepted leads, honoring each partner's **Exclude Returned Leads from Payout** setting |
| **Contribution Margin** | Net revenue minus payouts, with the share of revenue you keep as a percentage |
| **Rev / Received** | Net revenue divided by every submission partners sent, rejected ones included |

The table lists each partner's **Leads** and whether a **Ping** and **Post** are configured. Search by name, or use **Filters** to include archived partners.

## Creating and editing a partner

Click **New partner**, fill in the form, and click **Create partner**. To change a partner later, open it and click **Edit**.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-create-light.webp" alt="New partner form with Basic Info, Duplicate checks, Lead Quotas, and Toggles sections" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-create-dark.webp" alt="New partner form with Basic Info, Duplicate checks, Lead Quotas, and Toggles sections" />

| Section | Fields |
|---------|--------|
| **Basic Info** | **Name**, and an optional **Offer Penalty** (0 to 100%). The penalty lowers the partner's offer score when they compete for a lead, without changing what they're charged. Leave it empty for no penalty. |
| **Duplicate checks** | **Use tenant default**, **Disabled**, or **Custom**. See [Duplicate checks](#duplicate-checks) below. |
| **Lead Quotas** | Daily, weekly, and monthly limits on **Lead Count** and **Revenue**. See [Lead quotas](#lead-quotas). |
| **Toggles** | **Show Returned Status in Portal** lets the partner see when a lead was returned. **Exclude Returned Leads from Payout** leaves returned leads out of the payout total shown to the partner. |

### Duplicate checks

Duplicate checks stop you from sending a partner a lead they've already received. They compare against the partner's own delivery history: the window starts when a lead is delivered, returned deliveries and Club claims count, and pending reservations don't.

- **Use tenant default** follows the partner default under **Ingest → Duplicate Checks**.
- **Disabled** turns checks off for this partner.
- **Custom** replaces the default with this partner's own **History window**, **Field matching** (all or any of the selected fields), **Fields**, and **Matching options** (case sensitivity and punctuation). You can also choose whether a lead skipped as a duplicate can still go to the **Marketplace** or **Club**: immediately, never, or after a number of days.

See [Duplicate detection](/features/duplicate-detection) for how duplicate checks work across Juiced.

### Lead quotas

Lead quotas cap what you send to a partner. Set daily, weekly, and monthly limits for lead count and revenue; leave any field blank for no limit. Limits use totals so far, so the final lead can take the revenue total over the limit.

Once a partner reaches any quota, Juiced stops sending them new pings and posts: they drop out of highest-price and round-robin selection, and the limit is checked again right before each outbound ping or post. Leads keep flowing to other eligible partners, the marketplace, or fallback stages. Quotas reset each day, week, or month.

## The partner record

Open a partner to see its record. The tabs across the top are **Overview**, **Payouts** (for roles that can see analytics), **Lead Eligibility**, **Sources**, and **Users**.

  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-overview-dark.webp" alt="Partner overview with Pings, Posts, Leads, and Payout stats, a Ping/Post table listing a ping and a post endpoint, and a Details panel with Offer Penalty, Show Returned Status, Exclude Returned from Payout, and Created" />

The **Overview** shows **Pings**, **Posts**, **Leads**, and **Payout** for the timeframe you pick in the header, plus a **Details** panel with the partner's settings and any quotas. Below that are three tabs:

- **Ping/Post**: the partner's ping and post endpoints. See [Ping/post endpoints](#pingpost-endpoints).
- **Leads**: leads assigned to this partner, with **Status**, **Payout**, and **Date**. Filter by status.
- **Outgoing logs**: every ping and post attempt sent to this partner, with its result and price. Click a row for the full request and response.

### Archiving and deleting

From the page header:

- **Archive** removes the partner from active lists and lead distribution. Existing leads keep the partner's name for reference, and you can bring it back with **Restore**.
- **Delete permanently** is only offered for archived partners. It can't be undone: the partner is removed, detached from its leads and invites, and its offers are deleted.

## Ping/post endpoints

To sell leads to a partner, give them a ping, a post, or both:

- A **ping** runs before a lead is posted. It asks the partner for a price and whether they want the lead.
- A **post** delivers the lead to the partner once they win it.

On the **Ping/Post** tab, click **Configure Ping** or **Configure Post**. Each partner has at most one of each, so the button disappears once that endpoint exists.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-ping-config-light.webp" alt="Edit Ping page with Destination URL, collapsed Advanced settings showing no auth, POST, JSON, 10s timeout, and one header, a Payload mapping, and the start of the Response section" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-ping-config-dark.webp" alt="Edit Ping page with Destination URL, collapsed Advanced settings showing no auth, POST, JSON, 10s timeout, and one header, a Payload mapping, and the start of the Response section" />
</Frame>

| Section | What you set |
|---------|--------------|
| **Destination** | The **URL** the ping or post is sent to |
| **Advanced settings** | **Method** (POST, PUT, or GET), **Authentication** (None, Basic Auth, or Bearer Token), **Format** (JSON or Form), **Timeout** in seconds, and extra **Headers**. The defaults suit most endpoints. |
| **Payload** | The fields sent. Use **Load default mapping** for the standard shape, build rows yourself, or **Paste JSON** from the partner's spec. Keys use dot notation for nesting, and you can click a field to chain transformers. |
| **Response** | How to read the partner's reply. **Price path** and **Message path** point to the price and any message or rejection reason (dot notation, like `data.offer.price`). A **Success Condition** is required; a **Duplicate Condition** is optional. Conditions can check the **HTTP Status Code**, whether a key equals or contains a value (**Key Equals**, **Key Contains**), or whether the response contains some text (**Response Contains**). |

A new endpoint is saved with **Create & continue to testing**, which takes you to the edit page with a **Testing** section. Send a test using **Generate sample data**, **Add test data manually**, or **Test with a real lead**, and check the request, response, parsed price, and message before going live. Save later changes with the floating save bar.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-post-config-light.webp" alt="Edit Post page for a partner with the post Destination URL, Advanced settings, and Payload mapping" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-post-config-dark.webp" alt="Edit Post page for a partner with the post Destination URL, Advanced settings, and Payload mapping" />
</Frame>

Back on the **Ping/Post** tab, each endpoint's row shows its URL, method, format, and whether it's **Active** or **Paused**. Row actions let you pause or enable it, **Edit** it, or **Delete** it. A paused endpoint isn't called.

## Payouts

The **Payouts** tab helps you check what you owe a partner before you pay. Pick a timeframe in the header to see **Payout Owed** (for accepted leads, honoring the exclude-returned setting) and **Expected Revenue** (what customers were charged for the same leads), then a line-by-line list of leads with **Received**, **Status**, **Payout**, and **Revenue**. Everything is counted by the date each lead was received.

The **Sources** tab lists the sources assigned to this partner, with each one's **Type**, **Lead Category**, **Test Mode**, and **Payout %**. Click **Assign source** to attach one; only sources not already assigned to a partner are offered. Use **Unassign** on a row to detach it.

## Users

Partners can have their own users who sign in to the partner portal to see their leads and performance. The **Users** tab lists them with **Email**, **Authentication**, and **Joined on**.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-users-light.webp" alt="Partner Users tab listing partner users with email, authentication method, and joined date, and an Invite user button" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-users-dark.webp" alt="Partner Users tab listing partner users with email, authentication method, and joined date, and an Invite user button" />
</Frame>

- **Invite user**: enter an email and send. The invitee gets an email to accept and create their account, and joins as a partner admin with full access to the partner account.
- **Pending** invites show in the same list with **Resend invite** and **Revoke invite**. A revoked invite stops working immediately.
- **Reset 2FA and passkeys** helps a partner user who's lost their authenticator app or passkey.
- **Remove** takes away their access right away.
