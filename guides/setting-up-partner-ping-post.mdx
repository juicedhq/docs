---
title: Setting Up Partner Ping/Post
description: Connect a buyer partner's ping and post endpoints, map the payload, read their responses, test, and watch the deliveries.
---

Ping/post lets you sell leads to an outside buyer in real time. Juiced pings the partner with a preview of the lead and asks what they'll pay; if their offer wins, Juiced posts the full lead to them. This guide connects both endpoints for one partner. For the partner record itself, see [Partners](/features/partners).

## Prerequisites

- The **Admin** [role](/features/roles).
- A [partner](/guides/adding-a-partner) to connect.
- The partner's integration spec: ping and post URLs, method, authentication, the fields they expect, and what their responses look like for accepted, rejected, and duplicate leads.

## Step 1: Configure the ping

Open the partner, stay on the **Overview** tab, and in the **Ping/Post** tab click **Configure Ping**.

1. **Destination**: enter the partner's ping **URL**.
2. **Advanced settings**: expand it if the partner needs something other than the defaults. Set the **Method** (POST, PUT, or GET), **Authentication** (None, Basic Auth, or Bearer Token), **Format** (JSON or Form), **Timeout** in seconds, and any extra **Headers**. Saved passwords and tokens stay hidden after you save.

## Step 2: Map the payload

Nothing is sent until you map it, so add every field the partner expects.

- **Load default mapping** fills in the standard set of fields. It replaces whatever you've mapped so far, so start with it.
- **Paste JSON** takes a sample from the partner's spec. Values that match your lead fields become linked field pills.
- **Add row** adds a single key. Use dot notation in keys for nested JSON (`contact.first_name`), and click a field pill to chain transformers, for example to reformat a phone number.

<Tip>
Pings usually carry less than posts. If the partner's spec leaves out contact details for the ping, leave them out of your ping mapping too.
</Tip>

## Step 3: Tell Juiced how to read the response

Under **Response**, use dot notation for nested paths (like `data.offer.price`):

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Price path** | Where the partner's price or payout sits in the response |
| **Message path** | Where a message or rejection reason sits |
| **Success Condition** | Required. How Juiced knows the partner accepted. Choose **HTTP Status Code** (like `200`), **Key Equals** or **Key Contains** (a key and value, like `status` equals `accepted`), or **Response Contains** (text anywhere in the response). |
| **Duplicate Condition** | Optional. How Juiced recognizes the partner saying they already have this lead, for example **Response Contains** `duplicate`. |

Click **Create & continue to testing**.

## Step 4: Test it

The page reopens with a **Testing** section. Choose how to build the test:

- **Test with a real lead**: search your leads and send exactly what this partner would receive.
- **Generate sample data**: fake but realistic values for every mapped field.
- **Add test data manually**: build the payload one field at a time.

Click **Run Webhook Test**. The result shows whether the response was **Accepted** or **Rejected**, the status code and time taken, the parsed **Price** and **Message**, and the full **Request** and **Response**. If the price or message is missing, adjust the paths in **Response**, save with the floating save bar, and test again.

## Step 5: Configure the post

Back on the partner, click **Configure Post** and repeat Steps 1 to 4 with the partner's post URL and the full set of lead fields they need.

## Step 6: Turn on partners in distribution

Endpoints only get called when the partner can compete for a lead. In a [distribution stage](/features/distribution), turn on **Enable Partners** and include this partner. The partner's [lead eligibility](/features/partners#lead-eligibility), quotas, and duplicate checks all apply.

## Step 7: Watch the deliveries

On the **Ping/Post** tab, click the ping or post row to open its deliveries page.

  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-deliveries-light.webp" alt="Partner endpoint deliveries page with total deliveries, success rate, failed, average duration, and last delivery stats, a list of live deliveries, and the endpoint details" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/partners/partner-deliveries-dark.webp" alt="Partner endpoint deliveries page with total deliveries, success rate, failed, average duration, and last delivery stats, a list of live deliveries, and the endpoint details" />

Watch **Success rate** and **Failed** for the first few hours. Filter by **Result** to find failures, click one to see the request and response, and use **Redeliver** once the problem is fixed. For a wider view of every attempt to this partner, use the partner's **Outgoing logs** tab.

## Pausing an endpoint

If the partner asks you to stop, use the pause action on the endpoint's row, or **Pause ping** / **Pause post** in the header of its deliveries page. Paused endpoints aren't called until you enable them again. To cap volume instead of stopping it, set a [lead quota](/features/partners#lead-quotas).
