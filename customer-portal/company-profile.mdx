The Company Profile holds the details of the business you're buying for: its name, contact information, and address. Each business you belong to has its own. For your personal login settings, see [Profile and security](/customer-portal/account-settings).

To open it, click the gear icon (**Company Profile**) in the [business rail](/customer-portal/getting-around#the-business-rail), or go to **User Settings** in your user menu and choose the **Company profile** tab. You can also search for "Company Profile" with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>.

You'll only see the gear icon and the **Company profile** tab if your role lets you edit the company profile. If you don't see them, ask your account admin or provider for access.
</Note>

## Company details

- **Company name**: your business's name as it appears on your account.
- **Email**: your business's email address.
- **Phone**: your business phone number. US and Canadian formats are accepted.

## Business address

- **Address** and **Apt/Suite**
- **City**
- **Country**: United States, Canada, or Australia
- State (or province) and ZIP (or postal code). These labels change to match the country you select.

Save each section when you're done.
