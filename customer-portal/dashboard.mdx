The Dashboard is the first page you see after logging in. It greets you by name ("Good morning", "Good afternoon", or "Good evening", depending on your local time) and shows a quick snapshot of what's fresh.

## Latest leads

Your five most recently received leads, newest first:

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Lead** | The lead's name and lead type. A green **New** badge marks leads received in the last 24 hours. |
| **Contact** | Phone number and email. Click either to call or email, or use the copy button next to it. |
| **Location** | Where the lead is located |
| **Received** | How long ago you got the lead. Hover for the exact date and time. |
| **Price** | What you paid |

Click a row to open the lead's full details, or **View all →** to go to [Leads](/customer-portal/leads).

## Recent bids

Your five most recently updated bids:

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Bid** | The location the bid covers, with its state (or "Statewide" / "Nationwide") underneath |
| **Amount** | Your bid amount |
| **Position** | Where you stand against other bidders: **Top**, **Tied**, **Under**, or **Out** |
| **Lead Type** | The lead type the bid is for |
| **Status** | Whether the bid can win leads right now |

Click a row to find that bid on the [Bids](/customer-portal/bids) page, or **View all →** to see them all. [Bid position](/customer-portal/bids#bid-position) and [Bid statuses](/customer-portal/bids#bid-statuses) explain what each badge means.

If you haven't placed a bid yet, this section says **No bids yet**. A good nudge to go [place your first one](/customer-portal/bids#creating-a-new-bid).

## The webhook reminder

Until your account has a webhook, the top of the dashboard shows a short reminder: **Using a CRM? Set up a webhook** to send new leads there automatically the moment they arrive. Click the link to [create a webhook](/customer-portal/webhooks), or click the **×** to hide the reminder in this browser.
