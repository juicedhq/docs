Banners are announcements that run across the top of the customer portal, like a maintenance window, a new Club drop, or a nudge to top up. You write them once, schedule them, and optionally target them so only the right customers see them. Click the gear in the rail and choose **Banners** under **Application**.

## The banner list

Each banner shows its **Order**, **Headline**, **Style**, whether it's **Active**, and its **Publish** and **Until** dates. Filter by **Status** (active or inactive), or include deleted banners to restore one.

Order matters. Use **Move up** and **Move down** to rank banners. The customer portal shows one banner at a time, which is the first one in this order that's active, inside its schedule, and matches the customer's conditions.

## Creating a banner

Click **New banner**. A live preview at the top of the form shows the banner's style, headline, and button as you type.

### Banner Content

| Field | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| **Desktop Headline** | Required. The full message. |
| **Mobile Headline** | Required. A shorter version for small screens, where the full headline may not fit. |
| **Call to Action** | Optional button text, like "Browse the Club". |
| **Link** | Where the button goes. The button only shows when you fill in both the call to action and the link. |
| **Style** | **Primary**, **Warning**, **Alert**, or **Info**. Match it to the urgency. |

### Status & Visibility

| Field | What it does |
|-------|--------------|
| **Active** | The on/off switch. Turn it off to pull a banner without deleting it. |
| **Dismissable** | Customers can close the banner. Turn it off only for messages they genuinely can't miss. |
| **Publish At** | Required. When the banner starts showing. Set a future time to schedule it. |
| **Display Until** | When it stops. Leave blank to show it indefinitely. |

### Conditions

Leave **Conditions** empty to show the banner to every customer. To target a group, build conditions on customer attributes, such as the customer's state, country, balance, available budget, or the user's role. Conditions within a group must all match (AND), and the banner shows if any group matches (OR).

Conditions are checked each time a customer loads the portal, so a customer who starts matching later (say, their balance drops) sees the banner then without you changing anything.

Click **Create banner** to save. To change a banner later, open it and click **Edit**. Changes save with the floating save bar.

## Banner performance

Open a banner to see how it's doing.

  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/settings/banner-detail-light.webp" alt="Banner detail with Total Views, Unique Views, Total Clicks, Engagement, Dismissals, and Dismissal Rate stats, the banner content, its status and schedule, and targeting conditions marking it as a global banner" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/settings/banner-detail-dark.webp" alt="Banner detail with Total Views, Unique Views, Total Clicks, Engagement, Dismissals, and Dismissal Rate stats, the banner content, its status and schedule, and targeting conditions marking it as a global banner" />

| Stat | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Total Views** | Every time the banner was shown |
| **Unique Views** | Customers who saw it at least once |
| **Total Clicks** | Clicks on the call to action |
| **Engagement** | Clicks as a percentage of total views |
| **Dismissals** and **Dismissal Rate** | How often customers closed it, and as a percentage of total views |

The page also shows the banner's content, whether it's dismissable, its schedule, and its targeting conditions. A banner with no conditions is labeled a global banner.

## Deleting and restoring

Use **Delete** on a banner's page to remove it. Deleted banners stop showing but can be found again by including deleted records in the list filter, where you can **Restore** them or **Delete forever**.
