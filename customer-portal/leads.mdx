The Leads page lists every lead assigned to your account: leads you won with bids, bought in the [Marketplace](/customer-portal/marketplace), or claimed from your provider's [Club](/customer-portal/club), including any that were later returned. It's your purchase history and the fastest way back to a lead's contact details.

## The leads table

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Date** | When you received the lead |
| **Lead** | The lead's name and lead type. A green **New** badge marks leads from the last 24 hours. |
| **Contact** | Phone and email. Click to call or email, or use the copy button beside each. |
| **Location** | Where the lead is located |
| **Status** | **Assigned** (yours), **Returned** (returned to your provider), or **Club** (claimed from the Club) |
| **Price** | What you paid. A green price with a struck-through amount means a discount was applied; hover it to see the discount percentage and the price you were competing at. |

Sort by **Date**, **Lead** (last name), or **Price** by clicking the column header. At the end of each row, the copy button copies a tidy summary of the lead (name, lead type, phone, email, address, and received time), ready to paste into a text or another tool.

You can show 10, 25, or 50 leads per page.

## Searching and filtering

Use **Search leads** to find a lead by its ID, name, email, phone number, or any part of its address (street, city, state, ZIP, or country).

Click **Filters** to narrow the list:

- **Lead type**: one lead type at a time.
- **Status**: **Assigned**, **Returned**, or both.
- **Test mode**: show only test leads. Useful when you're checking that a [webhook](/customer-portal/webhooks) or notification is set up correctly.
- **Price**: a minimum and maximum in dollars.
- **Received**: a date range.

Each active filter appears as a chip above the table. Remove a chip to clear that filter.

## Lead details

Click any lead to open its details page.

  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/portal/leads/lead-detail-light.webp" alt="Lead detail page with Contact and Lead Details sections and a Purchase panel" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/portal/leads/lead-detail-dark.webp" alt="Lead detail page with Contact and Lead Details sections and a Purchase panel" />
</Frame>

- **Contact**: phone, email, and address, each with a copy button. The address has a **Maps** link, and a map of the location may appear alongside it.
- **Lead Details**: the extra fields your provider delivers with this lead type, such as project details or timeframes.
- **Purchase**: the **Lead type**, what you **Bought for**, the **Status**, the **Scope** you won it at (county, state, or country), when it was **Received**, and the **Lead ID** with a copy button.

## Exporting leads

Click **Export** at the top of the page to download your leads as a CSV. The export uses whatever search and filters you have applied, so filter first if you only want a slice.

Juiced builds the file in the background, so you can keep working. When it's ready, a **Lead export complete** notification appears under the [notifications bell](/customer-portal/getting-around#notifications-bell). Click it to download the file. Big exports take a little longer to press, but they won't tie up your browser.

## Returned leads

Leads you've returned stay in your list with the **Returned** status. To find them quickly, filter by **Status: Returned**.

You don't return leads from this page. Your provider handles returns through their own process, usually a **Lead Returns** link in the sidebar's Support group. See [Returns and your return rate](/customer-portal/returns).
