Leads are the fruit of your labor. Literally everything in Juiced exists to get leads from point A (your sources) to point B (your customers) while making sure you get paid along the way.

A lead is a person who's expressed interest in something—a quote, a service, a product. Your job is to connect that person with a customer who can help them. Juiced handles the messy middle: validation, routing, bidding, delivery, and the occasional dispute.

## Lead statuses

Every lead has a status that tells you where it is in its journey. Here's what each one means:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Pending** | Just arrived. Waiting to be matched with a customer. |
| **Processing** | Actively moving through your [distribution stages](/features/distribution). |
| **Assigned** | Delivered to a customer or partner. |
| **Posted** | Sent out of the system to a partner via an external post. |
| **Duplicate** | Matched an existing lead. Rejected to avoid double-selling. |
| **Returned** | Returned for a refund. Maybe the phone number was fake, maybe the lead wasn't what the customer expected. |
| **Unassigned** | Couldn't find a buyer. No matching bids, or everyone's out of budget. |
| **Withheld** | On hold because you've hit your [plan's lead limit](/features/billing). Will process automatically once capacity frees up. |
| **Marketplace** | Available for customers to claim on the [marketplace](/features/marketplace) rather than being pushed to a specific buyer. |
| **Club** | Available for subscribed customers to claim through your [Club](/features/club). |
| **Audit** | Held for manual review by an [Audit stage](/features/audits), or submitted as test data. |
| **Uncategorized** | Doesn't match any lead type, and no fallback was configured to catch it. |
| **Distribution Error** | Something went wrong during distribution. The lead can be reprocessed once the issue is resolved. |

A lead is considered "sold" if it's Assigned, Posted, or Returned. Yes, even returned leads count as sold—you delivered them, and the customer chose to return them afterward.

### Status tabs

The tabs across the top slice the list by status: **All**, **Assigned**, **Unassigned**, **Duplicates**, **Returned**, **Withheld**, **Pending**, **Marketplace**, **Club**, **Audit**, and **Uncategorized**. Each tab shows a count that respects your current search and filters, so the number tells you exactly how many rows you'll get when you click it. Tabs with nothing in them slide to the end of the row so the statuses that matter stay in view. Hover a tab for a one-line description of what it holds.

### Searching and filtering

The **Search leads** box matches names, lead IDs, emails, phone numbers, addresses, customers, states, postal codes, and statuses.

Click **Filters** to narrow things down further. You can filter by:

- **Lead types**, **States**, and **Counties** (multi-country workspaces see their regional labels instead)
- **Sources**, **Customers**, and **Partners**
- **Assigned scope** (County, State, Nationwide, Marketplace, or Posted)
- **Test mode** (real leads by default, or test leads only)
- **Value** range and **Created** date range

## Viewing a lead

Click any row to open the lead. The header shows the lead's name (or "No name provided"), its status, and a **Test Mode** badge for test leads.

- **Contact** — Phone, email, and address with one-click call, email, copy, and **Open in Maps** buttons. A map preview appears next to the address when your workspace has maps configured.

- **Deliveries** (sidebar) — Each customer or partner who received the lead, with the amount charged, the delivery's status, the scope it was won at (County, State, and so on), and the payout owed to the source. Returned deliveries show the amount struck through. If nobody has received it yet, you'll see "This lead hasn't been delivered to anyone yet."

### Field visibility

Not all fields are visible to everyone:

- **You** see all fields on a lead
- **Customers** only see fields marked as "deliverable" in the [lead category](/features/lead-categories) configuration

This lets you collect data for your own purposes (like tracking or analytics) without exposing it to buyers.

## Lead actions

Everything you can do with a lead lives in the **More actions** (⋯) menu in the lead's header, plus an **Edit** button when the lead can still be edited. You'll only see the actions your role allows and that make sense for the lead.

  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/leads/lead-actions-menu-light.webp" alt="More actions menu open on a lead, listing History, View Log, View Ping, View Post, Process, Trigger Webhook, Return, and Delete" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/leads/lead-actions-menu-dark.webp" alt="More actions menu open on a lead, listing History, View Log, View Ping, View Post, Process, Trigger Webhook, Return, and Delete" />

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **History** | Opens a panel with the lead's activity: status changes, edits, and who made them. |
| **View Log** | Opens the lead's [incoming log](/features/logs#incoming-logs), showing every stage it went through. |
| **View Ping** / **View Post** | Opens the [ping or post](/features/logs#ping-and-post-inspectors) that created the lead. Only shown for leads that came in that way. |
| **Process** | Runs the lead back through your distribution stages. See [Reprocessing leads](#reprocessing-leads). |
| **Trigger Webhook** | Resends the lead to the customer's configured webhook endpoints. Assigned leads only. |
| **Return** | Returns the lead and refunds the customer. See [Returning leads](#returning-leads). |
| **Delete** | Permanently deletes the lead. |

### Returning leads

You can return a lead on a customer's behalf. When you confirm the **Return lead** dialog:

- The customer is refunded the charged amount
- The lead no longer counts against the customer's lead cap
- The customer retains access to the lead data
- The lead status changes to "Returned"

  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/leads/lead-return-dialog-light.webp" alt="Return lead confirmation dialog explaining that the customer will be refunded" />

### Triggering the customer's webhook

If a customer's system missed a lead (their endpoint was down, say), **Trigger Webhook** resends the lead to the customer's webhook endpoints. It's only available on assigned leads, and you can trigger it once per minute per lead.

### Editing leads

You can edit a lead that hasn't been assigned yet to correct data or fill in missing information. Click **Edit** in the lead's header. The edit page has three sections:

- **Contact Information** — Name, email, and phone
- **Address** — Street, city, state, and postal code
- **Details** — The lead's custom field values

Metadata like the source and timestamps can't be changed. Save with the bar that appears at the bottom of the page. When you save, Juiced reclassifies the lead into the right lead type based on the updated data.

### Reclassifying leads

Reclassifying re-runs lead type matching against the lead's current data. This is useful when:

- A lead type's conditions have changed
- You're troubleshooting classification rules and want to test against real lead data

Open the lead's edit page and click **Reclassify**, then confirm the **Re-run Classification** dialog. If the lead type changes, you'll see the old and new lead types. If it's already correctly classified, you'll be told no change was needed.

### Reprocessing leads

Reprocessing runs leads back through your [distribution stages](/features/distribution). This is useful when:

- A lead failed to assign due to temporary issues (distribution errors, no available bids)
- Lead data was corrected and needs to be re-evaluated
- A lead is being held by an [Audit stage](/features/audits) and you've reviewed it
- You want to retry distribution from a specific stage

Every reprocess asks for a **Starting stage**. The list shows your own stages by name, in order. The lead runs that stage and continues through every stage after it.

#### Single lead

Choose **Process** from a lead's **More actions** menu. This works for a lead in any status, including one flagged as a duplicate at intake. The one exception: a lead that's already been delivered must be returned first, so the recipient is refunded before the lead goes anywhere else.

#### Bulk reprocessing

Select leads in the list and click **Process** in the bar that appears. Pick a starting stage and confirm. Bulk reprocessing only picks up leads in these statuses, and skips any that have been delivered:

| Status | Can bulk reprocess? |
|--------|----------------|
| **Unassigned** | Yes |
| **Uncategorized** | Yes |
| **Pending** | Yes |
| **Distribution Error** | Yes |
| All other statuses | No |

When it's done, you'll see how many leads were queued.

<Note>
Reprocessing requires the **leads.reprocess** permission, at least one distribution stage, and a workspace that isn't restricted.
</Note>

### Deleting leads

**Delete** removes a lead entirely. Use this for test data or leads that should never have entered the system in the first place. Deleting can't be undone.

The lead's incoming log is preserved for historical reference. In the logs, the lead column shows a **Deleted** badge instead of the lead's name.

## Exporting leads

Click **Export** in the Leads header to download leads as a CSV. You can choose:

- **Export filtered leads** — Uses your current status tab, search, and filters. Disabled until you've applied at least one.
- **Export all leads** — Ignores the table filters and exports everything.

You can also select rows in the list and click **Export** in the bulk actions bar to export just those leads.

Exports include at most the 10,000 most recent matching leads. The file is built in the background; when it's ready, a **Lead export ready** notification lands in the [notification bell](/features/dashboard#notifications) with a download link.

Exports include **all** of your custom fields, regardless of whether they're marked as deliverable. That gives you full access to any data you've collected, including internal tracking fields customers never see.

## Test leads

Leads submitted through a source with [test mode](/features/sources#test-mode) enabled are marked as test data. These leads:

- Are hidden from the Leads list by default
- Can be viewed by setting the **Test mode** filter to test leads only
- Display a "Test Mode" badge on the lead page

[Deterministic test zips](/features/sources#testing-with-zip-codes) produce specific statuses depending on the zip code used. Leads submitted with any other zip code are forced to **Audit** status to prevent distribution.

Test leads let vendors verify their integration works correctly without affecting production data or customer billing.
