---
title: "Sending Leads from Other Tools"
description: "Step by step guides for sending leads into Juiced from forms, funnels, ad platforms, CRMs and other lead platforms."
---

Any tool that can send an HTTPS POST with a JSON body and an `Authorization` header can send leads to Juiced. Tools that can't do that connect through Zapier or Make. Each guide below covers the setup on that tool's side; for the Juiced side (sources, credentials, field names, test mode and logs), see [Sending leads to Juiced](/guides/sending-leads-to-juiced).

Browse every guide on the [Juiced integrations page](https://usejuiced.com/integrations).
