title: Publishing a Banner
description: Write a customer portal announcement, schedule it, target it at the right customers, and check how it performs.

Banners are the in-portal megaphone: a strip across the top of the customer portal for maintenance windows, promotions, and gentle nudges. This guide publishes one end to end. For every field and stat, see [Banners](/features/banners).

## Prerequisites

- The **Admin** [role](/features/roles).
- Your copy: a full headline, plus a shorter one for phones.
- Optionally, a button label and the URL it should open.

## Step 1: Start a new banner

Click the gear in the rail, choose **Banners** under **Application**, and click **New banner**. Keep an eye on the live preview at the top of the form; it updates as you type.

## Step 2: Write the content

1. **Desktop Headline**: the full message, for example "Scheduled maintenance Saturday 3 to 5 AM ET. The portal will be read-only."
2. **Mobile Headline**: the short version, for example "Maintenance Sat 3 to 5 AM ET".
3. **Call to Action** and **Link** (optional): the button text and where it goes. The button only appears when both are filled in.
4. **Style**: **Info** for announcements, **Warning** for "please act soon", **Alert** for "act now", **Primary** for promotions.

## Step 3: Schedule it

1. Leave **Active** on.
2. Set **Publish At** to when it should start. A future time schedules it.
3. Set **Display Until** to when it should stop, or leave it blank to keep it up.
4. Leave **Dismissable** on unless customers truly can't be allowed to close it, like an outage notice.

## Step 4: Target it (optional)

With no conditions, every customer sees the banner. To narrow it down, add a condition group under **Conditions** and pick a customer attribute, an operator, and a value. For example, a low-balance nudge might use **Customer Balance** less than 50. Every condition in a group must match; add another group for an "or".

Conditions are checked whenever a customer loads the portal, so customers start (or stop) seeing the banner as they start (or stop) matching.

## Step 5: Create and order it

Click **Create banner**. If **Publish At** has passed and the banner is active, matching customers see it on their next page load.

The portal shows one banner at a time, the first match in your list order. If you have several active banners, use **Move up** and **Move down** on the Banners list so the most important one wins. An outage notice should sit above a promotion.

## Step 6: Check it

Look at the portal as a test customer who matches your conditions. If the banner doesn't show, check that:

- **Active** is on
- **Publish At** has passed and **Display Until** hasn't
- the customer matches the conditions
- no other banner higher in the list matches the same customer
- the customer hasn't already dismissed it

After it's been up a while, open the banner to see **Total Views**, **Unique Views**, **Total Clicks**, **Engagement**, and how often customers dismissed it. A high dismissal rate with few clicks is a hint the message isn't landing.

## Retiring a banner

Turn **Active** off to pull it straight away, or set **Display Until** to let it expire on schedule. **Delete** removes it from the list; you can still find it by including deleted records in the filter and **Restore** it.

<Tip>
A few well-timed banners beat a constant stream. If customers learn the top of the page is always shouting, they stop reading it.
</Tip>
