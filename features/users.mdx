Your Juiced tenant can have multiple users, each with their own login and [role](/features/roles). To manage your team, click the gear in the rail and choose **Users** under **Account**.

The list shows each person's **Name**, **Email**, **Role**, the sign-in methods they use (**Authentication**), and when they **Joined on**.

## Inviting users

Tenant admins can add someone to the team.

1. Click **Invite user**.
2. Enter their **Email**.
3. Pick a **Role** (Admin, Customer Support, Sales, or Data Validation). It defaults to Customer Support.
4. Click **Send invitation**.

The invitee receives an email with a link to accept the invitation and create their account.

## Pending invites

Invitations that haven't been accepted yet appear in the same list with a <span className="tone-badge tone-warning">Pending</span> badge. Each one has two actions.

- **Resend invite** sends the email again
- **Revoke invite** cancels it. Revoked invites stop working immediately, so anyone who tries the old link is turned away.

## Managing existing users

Each user's row has three actions.

- **Edit role** changes what they can access and do
- **Remove** takes away their access to your tenant
- **Reset 2FA and passkeys** helps someone who's lost their authenticator app or passkey. You'll be asked to confirm your password.

To change several people's roles at once, tick their checkboxes and click **Change role**.

You can't edit your own role or remove yourself, so there are no actions on your own row. Ask another admin if you need a change.

Removing a user takes them off your tenant and its roles straight away, and disconnects any [AI assistants](/features/ai-assistants) they had connected.

## Your own profile

Open the user menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Profile** to manage your own account.

- **Profile information** holds your name, email, and phone. Your role is shown here but can only be changed by an admin.
- **Update password**
- Under **Sign-in methods**, email codes protect your account by default. Add a **Passkey** or an **Authenticator app** (with recovery codes) for stronger, faster sign-in.
- **Sessions** shows where you're signed in. Use **Sign out other sessions** to sign out every other browser and device.

## Roles

Roles determine what a user can see and do within your tenant. Juiced comes with four built-in roles.

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| <span className="tone-badge tone-info">Admin</span> | Full access to everything. Can manage users, billing, settings, and all platform features. |
| <span className="tone-badge tone-info">Customer Support</span> | Can view and manage customers, leads, bids, and transactions. Ideal for support staff. |
| <span className="tone-badge tone-info">Sales</span> | Read access to leads and customers, plus the ability to send invites. Built for your sales team. |
| <span className="tone-badge tone-info">Data Validation</span> | Limited to viewing and updating leads. Perfect for QA or data review tasks. |

For a detailed breakdown of what each role can do, see [Roles & Permissions](/features/roles).
