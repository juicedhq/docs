title: Managing Customer Finances

Every customer has a wallet that pays for their leads. Deposits go in, assigned leads take money out, and returns put it back. This guide covers the controls you have for moving money on a customer's behalf and keeping their wallet topped up. For the full list of transaction types, see [Transactions](/features/transactions). For fees and minimums, see [Payments & Deposits](/features/payments-and-deposits).

## Prerequisites

- The **Admin** or **Customer Support** [role](/features/roles). Both can add and remove funds.
- A customer to work with. See [Onboarding customers](/guides/onboarding-customers).
- Stripe connected and a card saved on the customer, if you want auto recharge.

## 1. Check the customer's funding

Click **Customers** in the sidebar and open the customer. On the **Overview**, the **Wallet** stat shows the current balance, and the **Funding** status card tells you where they stand.

| Funding | What it means |
|---------|---------------|
| <span className="tone-badge tone-success">Available</span> | The balance can cover lead purchases |
| <span className="tone-badge tone-warning">Limited</span> | Some bids are held back, because the balance covers their minimum but not the full bid amount |
| <span className="tone-badge tone-danger">Exhausted</span> | No bid can compete, because the balance doesn't cover any bid's minimum. The customer shows as <span className="tone-badge tone-danger">Inactive</span> in your lists. |

You don't set funding status yourself, because it follows the balance. To change it, move money.

## 2. Add funds

Open the **More actions** (⋯) menu in the header and choose **Add funds**.

  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/customers/customer-add-funds-light.webp" alt="Add funds dialog with Amount, Description, and Receipt URL fields" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/customers/customer-add-funds-dark.webp" alt="Add funds dialog with Amount, Description, and Receipt URL fields" />
</Frame>

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Amount** | Required, at least $1 |
| **Description** | Required. Why you're adding funds, for example "Wire transfer received 4/15" or "Goodwill credit". |
| **Receipt URL** | Optional. A link to the invoice, wire confirmation, or receipt. It shows as **View receipt** in the customer's activity. |

The balance updates right away and the deposit appears in the customer's transactions as **Funds Added By Tenant**. No card is charged, so use this for money you've collected some other way, or for credits.

<Tip>
Always fill in the receipt URL for real money. When someone asks about this deposit in six months, you'll have the answer one click away.
</Tip>

## 3. Remove funds

Choose **Remove funds** from **More actions** to take money back, for example after a mistaken credit or a refund you handled outside Juiced.

- **Amount** must be at least $1 and no more than the current balance.
- **Description** is required and **visible to the customer**, so write it for them ("Refund for duplicate deposit on 4/21").

The withdrawal is recorded as **Funds Subtracted By Tenant**. If you take out too much, add it back with **Add funds** and an explanatory description.

## 4. Read the transactions

Open the **Transactions** tab for every wallet movement, newest first, with totals for **Transactions**, **Deposits**, **Withdrawals**, and **Net** at the top.

Each row shows the event, such as **Lead Assigned**, **Lead Returned**, **Funds Added**, **Auto Recharge**, or **Checkout Bonus**. See [Transactions](/features/transactions) for what each one means.

## 5. Check the timeline

Transactions are the money log. The timeline is the account log, covering leads received, funds added or removed, pauses, and other changes. The **Activity** feed on the overview shows the latest few. Choose **Timeline** from **More actions** (or **View all** under the feed) for the full history.

## 6. Set up auto recharge

Auto recharge charges the customer's default card whenever their balance drops below a trigger, so they don't run dry mid-campaign. Customers can turn it on themselves under **Billing → Auto recharge** in their portal ([see their side](/customer-portal/billing#auto-recharge)), or you can set it for them.

1. On the customer, click **Edit**.
2. In **Auto Recharge**, turn on **Auto Recharge**.
3. Set the **Trigger Amount** (recharge when the balance falls below this) and the **Recharge Amount** (how much to add each time).
4. Save with the floating save bar.

If the balance is already below the trigger, the first recharge runs straight away. Each one is recorded as an **Auto Recharge** transaction, and any [minimum deposit and transaction fee](/features/payments-and-deposits) you've set for auto recharge applies.

<Warning>
You can only turn auto recharge on when Stripe is connected (otherwise you'll see **Auto recharge needs Stripe**), customer payments aren't disabled in Payments & Deposits, and the customer has an active card.
</Warning>

## 7. Check payment methods

The **Payment Methods** tab lists the customer's saved cards with their **Status** and **Expiration**. You can **Make default** (the default card is the one auto recharge uses) or **Remove** a card, but not add one. Customers add cards from their portal, so card details never pass through your hands.

## When a customer says they can't buy

Check these in order.

1. Check **Funding**. Is the wallet **Exhausted**? Add funds or get them to.
2. Check **caps and budgets**, because a full wallet doesn't lift a cap. Look for <span className="tone-badge tone-danger">Exhausted</span> on their bids. See [Budgets and caps](/features/budgets-and-caps).
3. Check **the bids themselves** on the **Bids** tab to make sure they're active and competitive.

Nine times out of ten, it's the wallet.
