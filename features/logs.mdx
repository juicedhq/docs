Every request that touches Juiced leaves a receipt. When a source pings or posts, Juiced records an **incoming log**. When Juiced pings or posts to a partner, or sends a lead to a customer's webhook, it records an **outgoing log**. Together they answer the two questions you'll ask most, "What did they send us?" and "What did we send them?"

Both live under **Entries** in the sidebar, next to **Leads**.

## Incoming logs

An incoming log (also called a distribution flow) is one run of the pipeline, covering a ping, a post, the lead it created, and every [distribution stage](/features/distribution) the lead visited. Open **Incoming logs** to see them all, newest first.

  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/logs/incoming-logs-dark.webp" alt="Incoming logs list with Source, Ping, Post, Lead, Honored, Recipient, and Sold For columns, a search box, and a Filters button" />
</Frame>

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Source** | The source that sent the traffic and when. A <span className="tone-badge tone-warning">Test</span> badge marks test traffic, and an <span className="tone-badge tone-danger">Error</span> badge marks a flow that hit a distribution error. |
| **Ping** | The ping's outcome, <span className="tone-badge tone-success">Matched</span>, <span className="tone-badge tone-neutral">No Match</span>, <span className="tone-badge tone-info">Pending</span>, or <span className="tone-badge tone-danger">Rejected</span>. Blank means there was no ping (a direct post). |
| **Post** | What happened when the full lead was posted. |
| **Lead** | The lead that was created, linked to its page. A <span className="tone-badge tone-danger">Deleted</span> badge means the lead was later deleted. |
| **Honored** | <span className="tone-badge tone-success">Honored</span> when a ping's accepted offer won the lead on the post. |
| **Recipient** | The customer or partner who received the lead. |
| **Sold For** | What the lead sold for. |

The search box matches source and recipient names. Click **Filters** to narrow the list by any of these.

- **Ping** status and **Source**
- **Has Ping**, **Ping Honored**, **Has Post**, **Has Lead**, and **Assigned** (each Yes or No)
- **Recipient Type** (Customer or Partner) and **Channel Type** (Bid, Customer, or Ping/Post)
- **Test mode** (real traffic by default, or test only)
- **Created** date range

Filters are kept in the page URL, so you can bookmark or share a filtered view.

### Inside an incoming log

Click a row to open it. The page title is the log's ID (it starts with `dflow_`).

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/logs/incoming-log-detail-light.webp" alt="Incoming log with an Inbound, Routing, and Outbound timeline, Details and Records in the sidebar, and an empty Outgoing logs table" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/logs/incoming-log-detail-dark.webp" alt="Incoming log with an Inbound, Routing, and Outbound timeline, Details and Records in the sidebar, and an empty Outgoing logs table" />
</Frame>

- **Timeline** has three lanes. **Inbound** holds what the source sent (the ping and post), **Routing** holds what Juiced did with it (lead created, each stage's result, who won), and **Outbound** holds pings and posts Juiced sent to partners. Each card describes its event in plain language and links to the related ping, post, or lead where there is one.
- **Details** shows the flow's **Status**, **Mode** (Live or Test), **Duration**, and when it was **Initiated**.
- **Records** links to the **Source**, **Lead**, **Recipient**, **Ping**, and **Post**, plus **Offers** (the number of offers made, with a **View** link).
- **Outgoing logs** lists every partner ping and post this flow triggered, with date, type, partner, result, and price.

### Offers

Click **View** next to **Offers** to see every offer the lead attracted. The dialog lists each bidder's **Name** (the winner is badged <span className="tone-badge tone-success">Winner</span>), **Channel**, **Bid**, **Effective** price, **Penalty** (hover it for the reason), **Score**, **Discount**, **Charged**, and **Status**. It's the fastest way to see why one buyer beat another. See [ping-post](/features/ping-post) for how offers are scored.

## Outgoing logs

**Outgoing logs** lists every request Juiced sent out, including pings and posts to [partners](/features/partners) and webhook deliveries. Rows are grouped by the incoming log they belong to, with a **View log** link to jump back to it.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/logs/outgoing-logs-light.webp" alt="Outgoing logs list grouped by log number, with date, type, partner, result, price, and a View log link on each row" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/logs/outgoing-logs-dark.webp" alt="Outgoing logs list grouped by log number, with date, type, partner, result, price, and a View log link on each row" />
</Frame>

The search box matches partner names and endpoint URLs. **Filters** cover these options.

- **Type** (Ping, Post, or Webhook)
- **Result** (Pending, Succeeded, Duplicate, Rejected, HTTP error, Timeout, Validation failed, or Error)
- **Partner**
- **Test mode** (real traffic by default, or test only)
- **Created** date range

Click a row to see the full exchange.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/logs/outgoing-log-detail-light.webp" alt="Successful outgoing post with the request payload, the partner's response, and Details in the sidebar" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/logs/outgoing-log-detail-dark.webp" alt="Successful outgoing post with the request payload, the partner's response, and Details in the sidebar" />
</Frame>

- **Request** shows what Juiced sent, as **Payload**, **Headers**, or **Raw body**.
- **Response** shows what the partner sent back, as **Body**, **Headers**, or **Raw body**. When something went wrong on our side of the wire, an **Error** tab shows the error class and message.
- **Details** lists **Result**, **Type**, **Mode**, **Partner**, **Price**, **Response code**, **Method**, **Format** (JSON or Form), **Duration**, **Timestamp**, and **Endpoint**.
- **Records** links to the incoming log.

## Ping and post inspectors

Pings and posts don't have their own list pages. You reach them from an incoming log (the **Ping** and **Post** links under **Records**, or **View ping** / **View post** on a timeline card), or from a lead's **View Ping** and **View Post** actions.

### Ping inspector

Shows exactly what a source sent to the [ping endpoint](/api-reference/endpoint/v1/ping) and what Juiced answered.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/logs/ping-inspector-light.webp" alt="Ping inspector with the request payload, a response body containing a price and Match found message, and Details and Records in the sidebar" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/logs/ping-inspector-dark.webp" alt="Ping inspector with the request payload, a response body containing a price and Match found message, and Details and Records in the sidebar" />
</Frame>

- **Request** shows the **Payload** and **Headers**, plus **Validation errors** when the ping was rejected.
- **Response** shows the **Body** and **Headers** Juiced returned, including the price offered.
- **Details** lists the response **Status** code, **Mode**, **Source**, **Method**, **Timestamp**, and ping **ID**.
- **Records** shows the **Source**, **Lead category**, **Lead type**, **County**, and **Postal code** the ping resolved to.

### Post inspector

A post (also called a [submission](/features/submissions)) is the full lead a source sent to the [leads endpoint](/api-reference/endpoint/v1/leads). Every post is stored as received, whether or not it became a lead.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/logs/post-inspector-light.webp" alt="Post inspector with the request payload, Juiced's response, and Details and Records in the sidebar" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/logs/post-inspector-dark.webp" alt="Post inspector with the request payload, Juiced's response, and Details and Records in the sidebar" />
</Frame>

- **Request** shows the **Payload** as received (even fields that don't map to anything in your lead category), plus **Validation errors** when the post was rejected.
- **Response** shows the **Body**, **Headers**, and **Raw body** Juiced sent back.
- **Details** lists **Status** (Pending, Accepted, or Rejected), **Mode**, **Source**, **Response code**, **Duration**, **Timestamp**, and **ID**.
- **Records** links to the **Lead** the post created, if any.

| Post status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| <span className="tone-badge tone-sky">Pending</span> | Still being processed. This is a brief, transient state. |
| <span className="tone-badge tone-success">Accepted</span> | Passed validation and produced a lead. |
| <span className="tone-badge tone-danger">Rejected</span> | Failed validation; no lead was created. Check **Validation errors** for the field-by-field reasons. |

<Tip>
Test traffic is hidden by default. When a vendor is testing their integration, set the **Test mode** filter to **Test only** on Incoming logs to find their requests.
</Tip>

## Permissions

Each log screen has its own permission, so you can give a support teammate access to the logs without access to everything else. Incoming logs, outgoing logs, pings, posts, and offers are each controlled separately in [roles](/features/roles).
