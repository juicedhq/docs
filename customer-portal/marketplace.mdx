The Marketplace lists leads you can buy right now. Bidding brings leads to you automatically; the marketplace lets you browse what's available and pick. It's the farmers' market to bidding's weekly produce box.

<Note>
You'll only see **Marketplace** in the sidebar if your provider sells leads this way.
</Note>

## How it works

Each lead in the marketplace is sold in a reverse auction, where the price starts high and drops in steps over time until the lead sells or the auction ends. You can buy at today's price, or wait for a better deal and risk someone else getting there first.

The lowest the price will go is the **floor**. When the price reaches the floor, the auction ends. The lead goes to whoever holds the highest buy order, or leaves the marketplace if nobody placed one.

## What you'll see

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Location** | The lead's county and state |
| **Lead Type** | The lead type. Hover for a description, if your provider wrote one. |
| **Received** | How long ago the lead came in |
| **Ends in** | Time left in the auction |
| **Current** | The buy-now price at this moment |
| **Next drop** | The next, lower price and roughly when it kicks in. **Last chance!** means there are no more drops; **Ended** means the auction is over. |
| **Floor** | The lowest price the auction will reach. Its color shows where buy orders stand (see [Watching the floor](#watching-the-floor)). |

The table updates live as leads are added, sold, and repriced, so there's no need to refresh.

### Finding leads

- **Search** by county or auction ID.
- **Filter** by **State**, **County**, and **Lead type** (pick as many as you like). Use **My buy orders** to see only auctions where you have a buy order, and **My exceeded buy orders** to see the ones where someone has outbid you.
- Sort by **Location**, **Received**, **Ends in**, **Current**, or **Floor**.

Nothing that fits? The prompt under the table, **Didn't find what you are looking for? Place a bid**, takes you to [Bids](/customer-portal/bids) so matching leads come to you automatically next time.

## Buying a lead now

Click **Buy now** on a row, check the details, and confirm with **Buy for $X**. Your wallet is charged immediately and the lead lands in your [Leads](/customer-portal/leads) list.

### Buying several at once

Tick the checkboxes beside the leads you want, then click **Buy leads** in the bar that appears. The confirmation shows the total at current prices; click **Buy N leads** to buy them all. Prices keep dropping while you shop, so the confirmation shows what you'll pay at that moment.

## Placing a buy order

A buy order says "I'll take this lead at this price." It's how you wait for a lower price without staring at the countdown.

1. Click **Place buy order** on a row.
2. Choose an **Amount** from the list of remaining price steps.
3. Click **Place buy order**.

Your buy order becomes the new floor. The price stops dropping there, and if nobody places a higher buy order before the auction ends, you get the lead at your price. Each new buy order has to beat the current highest one.

The buy-order amount is **taken from your wallet when you place it**. You'll see it in [Transactions](/customer-portal/billing#transactions) as **Placed Buy Order**. You get it back as **Canceled Buy Order** if you cancel, or **Released Buy Order** when the auction ends without your order winning. If you win, the amount is released and the lead is charged at your buy-order price.

Changed your mind? Click **Cancel buy order** on the row while the auction is still running. You can only have one buy order per lead, so to go higher, cancel and place a new one.

## Watching the floor

The **Floor** badge color tells you where you stand. Hover it for a reminder.

| Color | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| <span className="tone-badge tone-neutral">Gray</span> | There are no buy orders yet |
| <span className="tone-badge tone-info">Blue</span> | Another customer has placed a buy order, and you haven't |
| <span className="tone-badge tone-success">Green</span> | You have the highest buy order |
| <span className="tone-badge tone-danger">Red</span> | You have a buy order, but another customer has a higher one |

When someone outbids you, Juiced can let you know by email or text, and tell you again if your buy order becomes the highest once more. Turn on **Buy order exceeded** and **Buy order reinstated** for your [notification contacts](/customer-portal/notifications#choosing-what-each-contact-receives).

## Wallet requirements

Every marketplace purchase and buy order is paid from your wallet balance. Keep it topped up, or set up [auto recharge](/customer-portal/billing#auto-recharge), so you're not left squeezing the last drop out of an empty wallet when the perfect lead shows up.

<Note>
If your provider's own account has a billing issue, the marketplace shows a notice and the buy buttons are hidden until it's resolved. Buy orders you already placed are still processed normally.
</Note>

## The public marketplace

If your provider offers it, anyone can browse their marketplace at `/marketplace` without logging in. It shows the same live auctions, with a **Sign up to purchase** button instead of the buy buttons. Once you [sign up](/customer-portal/signing-up-and-logging-in) and log in, you can buy from the marketplace inside the portal.
