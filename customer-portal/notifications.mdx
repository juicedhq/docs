Notification contacts are the email addresses and phone numbers that hear about what's happening on your account: a new lead, an outbid, a paused account. Set them up so the right people get the right alerts, like new-lead texts going to your sales team's phones and account alerts going to the office inbox.

Find them under **Notifications** → **Contacts** in the sidebar. To send lead data into your CRM or another system instead, use [Webhooks](/customer-portal/webhooks).

<Note>
Contacts get emails and texts. The [notifications bell](/customer-portal/getting-around#notifications-bell) in the portal is separate: everyone who can log in to your business sees in-app versions of the same alerts there (new leads, outbids, approved returns and so on), plus your lead export results. Contact settings don't change what appears in the bell.
</Note>

The table shows each contact's **Value** (the email address or phone number), **Type** (**Email** or **Phone**), and **Name**. Search or filter by type to find one. Each row has **Edit contact** and **Delete contact** actions, and you can tick several rows and use **Delete** to remove them together.

## Adding a contact

1. Click **New contact**.
2. Choose the **Type**: **Email** or **Phone**. You'll only see the types your provider has set up; phone contacts receive text messages.
3. Enter the **Email address** or **Phone number**, and optionally a **Name** so you remember whose it is.
4. Check **Consent** to confirm you authorize your provider to send notifications to this contact.
5. Click **Create contact**.

If you see **Contacts can't be added yet** instead, either your provider hasn't turned on email or text notifications for your account, or your account is archived. Contact your provider to get notifications enabled.

## Choosing what each contact receives

Click **Edit contact** on a row to open the contact. Below its details is a switch for every notification type, so each contact can get exactly the alerts it needs. Click **Save contact** when you're done.

  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/portal/notifications/contact-edit-dark.webp" alt="Contact edit page with the email address and name, then switches for Lead assigned, Return approved, Outbid, Bid matched, and Under funded notifications" />

| Group | Notification | Sent when |
|-------|--------------|-----------|
| **Leads** | **Lead assigned** | A lead is assigned to you |
| | **Return approved** | A lead you returned is approved |
| **Bids** | **Outbid** | Another customer sets a higher bid |
| | **Bid matched** | Another bidder ties you as top bidder |
| | **Under funded** | Your wallet falls below an active bid amount |
| **Account status** | **Account paused** | Your account is paused |
| | **Account unpaused** | Your account is unpaused |
| | **Return tier updated** | Your return tier (badge) changes |
| **Marketplace** | **Buy order exceeded** | Someone places a higher buy order than yours |
| | **Buy order reinstated** | Your buy order becomes the highest again |

The **Marketplace** group appears only if your provider has a [marketplace](/customer-portal/marketplace). Your provider can also switch off individual notification types for everyone, so you may see fewer switches.

<Tip>
Not sure which to turn on? **Lead assigned** and **Under funded** are the two that keep leads flowing: one tells you a fresh lead has arrived, the other warns you before your bids run dry.
</Tip>
