title: Leads

---

The Leads page shows the leads assigned to your customer account, including any that were returned or disputed. It is your purchase history and your fastest route to the contact and deliverable data for a lead.

## Lead table

Use the search box to find a lead by its ID, contact information, or address. You can also filter by lead type, status, test mode, price range, and the date received. Sort the table by received date, lead name, or price when you need to work a particular slice of your history.

The table includes:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Name** | The lead's name and available contact details |
| **Lead type** | The type of lead |
| **Address** | The lead's location |
| **Price** | How much you paid for the lead |
| **Received** | When the lead was delivered to you |

Click a lead to view its contact information, address, custom deliverable fields, purchase price, assignment scope, received time, and lead ID. You can copy the lead ID or individual contact details from this page when handing the lead to another system or teammate.

## Exporting leads

Select **Export** to request a CSV of the leads in your current filtered view. Juiced prepares the file in the background and notifies you when it is ready to download, so large exports do not turn your browser into pulp.
