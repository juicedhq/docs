Customers are the businesses buying leads from you. They're the reason this whole operation exists: they want leads, you have them, and Juiced connects the two while making sure money changes hands appropriately.

## Customers vs. users

Before diving in, let's clear up a common point of confusion: **customers are not users**.

| Concept | What it represents | Example |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| **Customer** | A business entity | "Acme Insurance Co." |
| **User** | A person who logs in | "Jane Smith, Account Manager" |

A single user can access multiple customers (useful for agencies managing several accounts), and a single customer can have multiple users (the whole team can log in). Users authenticate. Customers purchase leads.

If your role includes analytics, the top of the page shows **Wallet Balance**, **Active Markets**, **New Customers**, and **Revenue** for the selected timeframe, with a revenue chart underneath. Change the period with the timeframe picker in the header.

Below that:

- **Status tabs**: All, Active, Paused, Inactive, and Archived, each with a count
- **Search**: by customer name or email, including the emails of users attached to the customer
- **Filters**: Auto recharge, Subscribed, Archived customers, Operational, Funding, Cap, and Budget status, and a wallet **Balance** range. You'll also see **Return Tier** filters when you use return tiers, and an **Affiliate** filter when you have [checkout page](/features/checkout-pages) affiliates.
- **Sorting**: by name, balance, active markets, leads, or created date

The table shows each customer's status, balance, active markets, lead count, return tier and return rate, whether auto recharge is on, their subscription, and when they were created.

## Creating and editing a customer

Click **New customer** to add a buyer. This creates the same kind of account as a customer registering through your portal; you're just doing it on their behalf. To change an existing customer, open them and click **Edit**.

The form has these sections:

| Section | Fields |
|---------|--------|
| **Basic Info** | Name, Business Email, Phone |
| **Address** | Street Address, Apt / Suite, City, Country (United States, Canada, or Australia), State, and ZIP. Street address, city, state, and ZIP are required. |
| **Toggles** | See [Account toggles](#account-toggles) below |
| **Bidding Limits** | Daily, weekly, and monthly lead caps and budgets. Each field only appears when you've enabled that limit for customers in **Settings → Bidding**; if none are enabled, the section is hidden. Leave a limit empty for unlimited. See [Budgets and caps](/features/budgets-and-caps). |
| **Auto Recharge** | Auto Recharge, Trigger Amount, and Recharge Amount. See [Auto recharge](#auto-recharge). |

Click **Create customer** to save a new customer. When editing, the floating save bar appears as soon as you change something.

### Account toggles

- **Can Access Bidding**: lets the customer place bids and view counties in their portal. This starts **off** for customers you create, so turn it on if they should bid for themselves.
- **Manual Club Access**: grants access to your [Club](/features/club) without a subscription, for example to comp a customer. An active subscription grants access automatically, independent of this toggle.
- **Disable Return Tiers**: exempts the customer from your [return tiers](/features/returns); their return rate shows instead of a tier. Only appears when you use return tiers.

Pausing isn't a form field; it lives in the customer's **More actions** menu.

### Auto recharge

Auto recharge tops up the wallet from the customer's default card whenever the balance falls below the **Trigger Amount**, adding the **Recharge Amount** each time. It's triggered immediately if the balance is already below the trigger when you turn it on.

You can only turn it on when:

- Stripe is connected (otherwise the section shows **Auto recharge needs Stripe**),
- customer payments aren't disabled in [Payments & Deposits](/features/payments-and-deposits), and
- the customer has an active payment method.

## The customer record

Click any customer to open their record. The header shows the customer's name and status, a timeframe picker, an **Edit** button, and the **More actions** (⋯) menu. Tabs underneath take you to everything about this customer: **Overview**, **Bids**, **Lead Eligibility**, **Leads**, **Transactions**, **Payment Methods**, **Contacts**, **Webhooks**, and **Users**.

### More actions

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Add funds** / **Remove funds** | Adjust the wallet by hand. See [Wallet management](#wallet-management). |
| **Pause** / **Resume** | Temporarily stop the customer's bidding without archiving them. |
| **Timeline** | Opens the customer's full activity history. |
| **Cancel subscription** | Only shown when the customer has a live subscription. See [Subscription products](/features/subscription-products#cancelling-a-customers-subscription). |
| **Archive** | Archives the customer and their bids. They can no longer receive leads or add funds. |
| **Reactivate** | Only shown on archived customers. Makes the account available again. |

### Archiving and reactivating

Archiving a customer also archives their bids. A customer with an active subscription can't be archived; cancel the subscription first (immediately, or wait for a scheduled cancellation to finish). To bring an archived customer back, open them and choose **Reactivate** from **More actions**.

## Overview

### Key metrics

If your role includes analytics, the overview opens with four stats:

| Metric | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Revenue** | Amount charged to this customer in the selected timeframe (four weeks by default), with the change from the previous period |
| **Wallet** | Current available balance |
| **Active Bids** | Number of bids currently running |
| **Return Rate** | Returned deliveries as a percentage of this customer's charged deliveries in the selected period |

### Status cards

Four status cards help you quickly diagnose why a customer might not be receiving leads. Hover the info icon on each for a description.

#### Operational

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | Bidding on and receiving leads. |
| **Paused** | Bidding is paused for this customer. |
| **Archived** | No longer operating. Historical data is preserved. |

#### Funding

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Available** | Balance can cover lead purchases. |
| **Limited** | Balance is running low. |
| **Exhausted** | Out of funds; can't buy leads. |

#### Cap

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Unlimited** | No cap on lead volume. |
| **Available** | Within the lead cap. |
| **Exhausted** | Lead cap has been reached. |

#### Budget

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Unlimited** | No spending limit set. |
| **Available** | Within the spending budget. |
| **Limited** | Approaching the budget limit. |
| **Exhausted** | Budget spent for this period. |

#### Display status

Rather than showing all four statuses everywhere, Juiced computes a **display status** that summarizes the customer's situation. It's the badge next to the customer's name and the status in the customer list.

| Display status | Shown when |
|----------------|------------|
| **Active** | Operational and has available funding |
| **Paused** | Manually paused |
| **Inactive** | Wallet funding is exhausted (no bid can cover its minimum) |
| **Archived** | Account archived |

<Note>
Display status only reflects operational and funding state. Cap and budget exhaustion show up on the customer's individual **bids** (as **Capped** or **Exhausted**) rather than changing the customer's own display status. A customer with every bid capped will still show as **Active** if their wallet is funded; they just won't be receiving leads.
</Note>

### Activity

The **Activity** feed shows the customer's eight most recent events: leads received, funds added or removed, pauses, and other account changes. Funds entries that have a receipt include a **View receipt** link. Click **View all**, or choose **Timeline** from **More actions**, to see the full history.

### Side panels

The right-hand column shows:

- **Details**: Customer ID, email, phone, address, and created date
- **Account**: return tier (when you use return tiers) and the customer's **Stripe ID**, which links straight to the customer in your Stripe dashboard
- **Club**: access source, subscription tier, billing status, renewal date, and claims used this period. See [Club](/features/club#customer-access-and-subscriptions).
- **Toggles**: the current state of each [account toggle](#account-toggles)
- **Registration Tracking**: for customers who registered through a tracked link, the registration URL, its UTM parameters, and the affiliate who referred them, if any

## Wallet management

Customers have a wallet balance that funds their lead purchases. You can add or remove funds without processing an actual payment, which is useful for credits, adjustments, or corrections. Both actions are in the **More actions** menu.

- **Add funds** asks for an **Amount**, a **Description** (the reason you're adding funds), and an optional **Receipt URL**.
- **Remove funds** asks for an **Amount** and a **Description**, which is visible to the customer. You can't remove more than the current balance.

Every adjustment shows up in the customer's [transactions](/features/transactions) and activity. For a step-by-step walkthrough, see [Managing customer finances](/guides/managing-customer-finances).

## Bids

The **Bids** tab lists every bid this customer has placed, with its **Status**, **My Bid**, the current **Top Bid**, the customer's **Position**, and the **Lead Type**. **Caps** and **Budgets** columns appear when you've enabled bid-level limits in **Settings → Bidding**.

Search by location, or filter by **Lead type**, **Scope** (county, state, or nationwide), and **Archived bids**. When a return-tier discount applies, **My Bid** is struck through: hover it to see what the customer actually pays. The bid still competes at full price.

Each row has **Edit**, **Pause** / **Unpause**, **History** (a sheet showing changes to the bid), and **Delete**.

Click **New bid** to bid on the customer's behalf. Search for a **Location** (one search box covers counties, states, and countries), pick a **Lead type** (only types with bidding enabled for that location's level are offered), and choose **My bid** from the amounts allowed, or enter a custom amount. Bid-level caps and budgets only appear when you edit an existing bid.

## Lead eligibility

Use the **Lead Eligibility** tab to control which leads this customer can receive. Build conditions on lead fields, **Lead Category**, **Lead Type**, or **Source**, then click **Save conditions**.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/customers/customer-eligibility-light.webp" alt="Customer Lead Eligibility tab with a condition builder for restricting which leads the customer receives" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/customers/customer-eligibility-dark.webp" alt="Customer Lead Eligibility tab with a condition builder for restricting which leads the customer receives" />
</Frame>

Conditions inside a group must all match (AND), and a lead only needs to match one group (OR). With no conditions, every lead qualifies. These checks apply to **bidding** and **round robin** delivery only; Marketplace purchases and Club claims are not affected.

<Note>
You need permission to update customers to open this tab. Buyers can't view or edit these conditions themselves.
</Note>

## Transactions

A record of every change to the customer's wallet, with totals for **Transactions**, **Deposits**, **Withdrawals**, and **Net** at the top. See [Transactions](/features/transactions) for the full breakdown of events.

## Payment methods

The **Payment Methods** tab lists the customer's saved cards with their **Status** and **Expiration**. Each card has:

- **Make default**: the default card is used for auto recharge
- **Remove**: detaches the card from the customer in Stripe. You can't remove the default card.

Customers add their own cards from their portal; there's no way to add a card from the tenant panel. Without Stripe connected, this tab shows **Cards need Stripe**, and you can adjust balances by hand instead.

## Contacts

Contacts are the email addresses and phone numbers that receive this customer's [notifications](/features/notifications). Each contact can have a name for easier identification.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/customers/customer-contacts-light.webp" alt="Customer Contacts tab listing contacts with value, type, and name" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/customers/customer-contacts-dark.webp" alt="Customer Contacts tab listing contacts with value, type, and name" />
</Frame>

Click **New contact**, choose **Email** or **Phone**, and enter the value. Each row has:

- **Test**: sends a test notification so you can confirm delivery
- **Edit**: change the contact and its notification preferences
- **Delete**: remove it (you can also select several and **Delete selected**)

Notification preferences appear when you edit a contact, grouped like this:

| Group | Notifications |
|-------|---------------|
| **Leads** | Lead assigned, Return Approved |
| **Bids** | Outbid, Bid matched, Under funded bids |
| **Account Status** | Account paused, Account unpaused, Return Tier Updated |
| **Marketplace** | Buy order exceeded, Buy order reinstated |

Each contact has its own settings, so you can route different alerts to different people or channels. Whether a message actually goes out by email or text also depends on your [notification templates](/features/notifications) and your SendGrid and Twilio [integrations](/features/integrations).

## Webhooks

Customer webhooks send lead data to an HTTP endpoint every time a lead is assigned to this customer. You and the customer can both manage them; customers do it from their portal.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/customers/customer-webhooks-light.webp" alt="Customer Webhooks tab listing webhooks with name, URL, method, status, and last delivery" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/customers/customer-webhooks-dark.webp" alt="Customer Webhooks tab listing webhooks with name, URL, method, status, and last delivery" />
</Frame>

Click **New webhook** and set up:

- **Destination**: an optional **Name** and the **URL** the lead data is sent to
- **Advanced settings**: **Method** (POST, PUT, or GET), **Authentication** (None, Basic Auth, or Bearer Token), **Format** (JSON or Form), **Timeout**, and extra **Headers**
- **Payload**: the **Default payload** (every available field in the standard shape) or a **Custom payload** where you pick fields, rename keys, add static values, or paste your own JSON shape

Click **Create & continue to testing**. On the edit page you can then test the webhook with a real lead, generated sample data, or test data you add by hand, and see the request and response.

Click a webhook to open its overview: delivery stats (total deliveries, success rate, failures, average duration, and last delivery), its settings, and a **Deliveries** list split into **Live** and **Tests**. Filter by result or date, open any delivery to see the request and response, and **Redeliver** it if needed. **Pause webhook** stops deliveries without deleting it.

## Users

The **Users** tab lists everyone who can sign in to this customer's portal, with their **Email**, **Authentication** methods, and **Joined on** date. Pending invitations appear in the same list with a **Pending** badge.

<Frame>
  <img className="block dark:hidden" src="/images/tenant/customers/customer-users-light.webp" alt="Customer Users tab listing users with email, authentication methods, and joined date, with an Invite user button" />
  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/customers/customer-users-dark.webp" alt="Customer Users tab listing users with email, authentication methods, and joined date, with an Invite user button" />
</Frame>

- **Invite user**: enter an email address and send an invitation. The person joins as an admin of this customer. Juiced doesn't email anyone when you create a customer, so this is how the first person gets in.
- **Resend invite** and **Revoke invite**: on pending invitations
- **Remove**: detach a user from this customer
- **Reset 2FA and passkeys**: for a user who's locked out of their sign-in methods. Only tenant admins see this, and you'll be asked for your password.
