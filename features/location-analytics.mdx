title: Location Analytics
description: Understand customer demand by geography to make smarter decisions about where to invest.

Location analytics shows you where your customers want leads—and where they're actually getting them. This data helps you make informed decisions about ad spend, source partnerships, and pricing strategy. If customers are bidding heavily in Miami but you're barely sourcing leads there, that's a gap worth closing.

The data is broken down at three levels: countries, states, and counties. Each level shows the same core metrics at a different geographic scope.

## Location overview

Open the **Locations** group in the sidebar and choose **Counties**, **States**, or **Countries**. You'll see a table with demand and performance data for each location.

Workspaces that operate in a single country see that country's own terms (for example, "ZIP Codes" in the US). Workspaces that operate in several countries see combined labels like **States / Territories** and **Counties / Postcodes**.

### Demand columns

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Active** | Count of fully operating bids at this level—customers who are ready to buy right now. On States, only state-wide bids count; on Countries, only nationwide bids. |
| **Top Bid** | The highest current bid amount in this location. |

Demand columns always reflect your bids as they stand right now.

### Lead performance columns

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Received** | On **Counties**, every lead received in the county. On **States** and **Countries**, only leads assigned at state-wide or nationwide scope. |
| **Min Value** | Lowest charged amount among those leads |
| **Max Value** | Highest charged amount among those leads |
| **Avg Value** | Average charged amount among those leads |

These columns cover the timeframe you pick in the header.

### Timeframe, filters, and search

Use the timeframe picker in the top-right to choose the period for the lead columns: **Billing period**, **Today**, **7D**, **4W**, **3M**, **12M**, **MTD**, **Last month**, **YTD**, **All**, or a **Custom** range. Your choice carries over between the Locations pages and the [Dashboard](/features/dashboard#choosing-a-timeframe).

By default, the table covers all lead types. Use the **Lead types** filter to narrow down to specific ones—useful if you want to see where demand is strongest for one product versus another. The Counties page adds a **State** filter, plus a **Country** filter for multi-country workspaces. Search by name to find a location quickly. Filters and sorting are kept in the page URL, so you can bookmark a view.

### Exporting

The **Counties** page has an **Export** button that builds a CSV of every county matching your filters. The export ignores the timeframe. When the file is ready, a **County export complete** notification arrives in the [notification bell](/features/dashboard#notifications) with a download link. States and Countries don't have an export.

## Individual location view

Click any location to see detailed information about that specific geography.

  <img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/tenant/locations/county-detail-dark.webp" alt="County page with Active Bids, Interested Customers, and Top Bid tiles, a Last 30 Days values row, a bids table, and the county's ZIP codes in the sidebar" />

### Overview metrics

- **Active Bids** — Bids ready to receive leads in this location
- **Interested Customers** — Customers with *any* bid on this location, including paused or otherwise inactive bids
- **Top Bid** — The highest bid amount, across all lead types or just the ones you've filtered to

The distinction between active bids and interested customers matters. Active bids tell you current demand. Interested customers tell you *potential* demand—people who've shown interest but might be paused due to budget, capacity, or other reasons.

### Values

Below the tiles, a row of values for the selected timeframe (the last 30 days unless you pick another): **Leads assigned**, **Minimum Value**, **Maximum Value**, and **Average Value**, each compared with the previous period of the same length.

### Bids

A table of the bids set at exactly this level: county bids on a county page, state-wide bids on a state page, nationwide bids on a country page. Each row shows the **Customer**, the customer's status, **Lead Type**, **Amount**, and the bid's status broken out into **Operational**, **Funding**, **Cap**, and **Budget**. Use **Filters** to narrow by lead type or any of the four status types, and search by customer.

### Child locations

The sidebar lists what's inside the location:

| When viewing | Shown in the sidebar |
|--------------|----------------|
| County | Its state and country, plus its postal codes |
| State | Its country, plus its counties |
| Country | No child list |
